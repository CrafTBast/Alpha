Chapter 967: Simultaneous Events (2)

Ikael clenched her fists.

It looked less like a battle stance and more like she was desperately holding onto something.

Eventually, she couldn't hold back her sobs.

"Hic."

It was like a miracle.

A dead child coming back to life to tell their mother it wasn't her fault.

"Ikael."

Satiel asked coldly.

"What happened? If it's true that you've regained your memories, you need to explain..."

Uriel interrupted.

"Wait."

Satiel glared at him with fierce eyes.

"What? Are you talking to me?"

"Whatever the lost memories are, it's already in the past. Ikael hasn't chosen anything yet."

"Nonsense."

Satiel snorted.

"The past? Ikael deceived us. She pretended not to know everything."

"Hey, you."

Shirone spoke with anger.

"Be quiet. I'm talking right now."

Though his anger was justified, Satiel, on the receiving end, was dumbfounded.

"Are you, a mere human, trying to match an archangel? Should I kill you right now?"

Ikael spoke.

"Satiel, Uriel is right. I will judge and decide."

"Ugh!"

Satiel felt a massive sense of humiliation rising from deep within. Goffin and Ikael, Shirone and Ikael. The latter relationship endlessly stimulated the lost memories of the former.

'I hate it. I hate it so much.'

The lack of clarity made it even more maddening, and finally, Satiel's reason evaporated.

"Shut up."

Ikael's eyes twitched.

Insulting the archangel was a rare event in Heaven's history.

Uriel turned with a murderous look, but Satiel's face was already twisted like a monster.

"Why? Who are you?"

It seemed out of context, but Shirone and Ikael understood perfectly.

"How great do you think you are to treat me like this?"

The purity disappeared from her halo, and tears of blood, not light, flowed from her eyes.

The three-horned mara, Ionas, spoke.

"Satiel, calm down. Remember the mechanism of a fallen angel."

Can an angel fall?

Perhaps it was possible if a pure spiritual being was imbued with a heart.

A heart that causes pain.

And a heart that torments oneself only emerges when one is prepared.

"What did I do to be ignored?"

Satiel didn't stop.

Every time Shirone and Ikael agreed, it felt like her mind was being torn apart.

'Yes, now I understand.'

It was a vague feeling, but.

"I was always a sideshow! You ruined me! Because of you!"

This time, Shirone also kept silent. He knew how much she had cared for humans before Goffin and Ikael had their child.

'Poor angel.'

But he also knew.

That she had driven a newborn baby, who hadn't even received a name, to a terrible death.

'You killed me.'

Satiel screamed.

"Tell me! What did I do wrong? You know! Did I trample on the archangel's authority? Or..."

Suddenly, she stopped speaking.

"Ah."

From the scattered fragments of memory, countless images emerged as if drawn by a magnet.

Anke Ra, secret conversations, the child's death.

'No.'

Her fingertips trembled, and her mind burned so hot it felt like her whole body was on fire.

"That can't be."

She tried to deny it, but delusions were impossible in the mind of an angel, a being of absolute reason.

"There must have been... a reason, right?"

Ikael didn't answer, and the longer her silence, the more Satiel went mad.

"No! It's because you were bad! Yes, that's it. It's all your fault!"

Ionas shouted.

"Calm down! If your mind collapses any further...!"

"Aaaaaaah!"

Satiel's halo lost its circular shape as she nervously grabbed her head.

"Kill!"

Her blood-soaked eyes targeted Shirone.

"Kill him!"

As soon as the order was given, Ionas disappeared and reappeared in front of Shirone.

Extreme Disintegration - Atomic Movement.

"Don't resent me."

Ionas's right arm disintegrated at the atomic level and shot forward at the highest speed in the physical world.

Enemy Zero.

A military term meaning an absolute safe zone.

It was also the state temporarily created inside Bashuka by Shirone's Hand of God.

"We won! We won!"

It was unclear where the information leaked from, but citizens came out into the streets and cheered.

The demons had been annihilated, and the giants, whose sheer size had caused shock, had disappeared.

"Long live Tormia! Long live Tormia!"

They were curious about the light of Agape that exploded in the sky, but no one spoke first.

For now, they just shouted, escaping the unpleasant truth that the war wasn't over yet.

"People of Tormia, listen!"

A carriage carrying a high platform stopped in the center of the square.

An old man, 17th in line to the throne, stood on the platform with a loudspeaker.

The citizens didn't know him, but seeing the royal attire adorned with Tormia's symbol, they knelt.

The square fell silent, and the stillness spread like ripples, eventually engulfing all the blocks.

"Dear people of Tormia, today, the great king of our great nation, Adolf XII, has passed away."

The citizens' eyes filled with shock.

"This can't be..."

The old man, who had briefly captured their attention, spoke.

"Remember only this, people. The royal family did not retreat an inch against the invaders of Bashuka."

In reality, it was because they had no information about the elemental bomb, and the old man ground his teeth in secret.

'How dare they bypass the royal family? Whoever is behind this, if I catch them, I won't let them go.'

He still couldn't grasp what was happening, but restoring royal authority was the top priority.

"King Adolf XII fought the giants to the end and died bravely."

"Oh, Your Majesty."

One of the crying citizens stood up.

"Then, could the light that defeated the demons be the light bestowed by the king as he passed away?"

The old man's eye twitched.

'What nonsense? How annoying.'

But even the most rational person seeks God when life is at stake.

"I think so too." The atmosphere became solemn.

"Even in his final moments fighting the giants, the king only worried about his people. And that is the royal family of Tormia."

"Wow!"

As the citizens cheered, the old man shouted loudly, riding the momentum.

"The great king's bloodline will never be broken! I officially declare, welcome Adolf XIII, the eldest son of Adolf XII, as the new king! Let's fight! We, Tormia, will never run away!"

"Long live King Adolf XIII! Long live Tormia!"

The citizens' cheers didn't stop, and their voices reached Alrog Street.

Lupist, struggling with documents in the Magic Association's president's office, frowned.

"It's so noisy."

When Plu closed the window, the noise subsided.

"The royal family is quick to act. It's actually lucky. Anyway, it seems there will be no problem with the succession."

"There better not be. Those guys can't do anything but eat."

Someone knocked hard on the door from outside.

"President! Lupist! Are you there? I need to talk to you! It's urgent!"

It was the voice of the 7th in line to the throne, and Lupist could guess why he was here.

The voice grew louder.

"Open the door! You knew Bashuka would be in ruins soon! How dare you deceive the royal family?"

Plu, who had his hands behind his back, pointed to the door and asked.

"What should we do?"

"Chase him away. I don't have time to wrestle with politicians."

Quickly assessing the damage and reorganizing the city's forces was the top priority.

Plu opened the door halfway and spoke.

"Sorry, the president is busy with official duties. Please come back later."

"What? You, the chief secretary? Do you know who I am? I'm the 7th in line to the throne... Ouch!"

The head of security approached and twisted the royal's arm.

"Excuse the rudeness. This is a special case due to the wartime situation, so please understand."

"It hurts! It hurts! Aaaah!"

As Plu sighed and watched, Shirone walked down the hallway.

"Let go! I said let go! It hurts!"

Shirone turned his head to follow the royal being dragged along the floor.

When he turned back, he had already reached the president's office.

"Sister, who was that person?"

Plu opened the door wide and smiled.

"Not an important person. Come in."

As Shirone walked into the president's office, Lupist stood up and asked.

"Quantum transmission?"

"Spatial movement. It's inconvenient if it's too close. Anyway, I'm familiar with Bashuka."

There were various landmarks.

"You were looking for me?"

"Yes. As you saw on your way here, the royal family has started to act. They will try to take all the credit for this war to ensure a stable succession. Including your achievements."

"It's okay. I don't mind."

"Of course. But it's uncertain if the current royal family can properly lead the wartime situation."

It was a provocative statement for the president to make, and Shirone blinked.

Lupist revealed his true intention.

"We want to support Pony."

"Hmm."

Shirone neither agreed nor disagreed but seemed to be thinking carefully.

"Is there a special reason?"

"She is one of the few who figured out the elemental bomb project on her own. I thought she would use this opportunity to stage a coup, but it seems she didn't."

Shirone chuckled.

"Because she's a magician."

"Exactly. That's why we need her. For the Ultima System project. If Pony becomes the king of Tormia, it will greatly help in uniting humanity."

It was an undeniable truth.

"Then what about the current king? Is he significantly lacking in qualifications?"

"Qualifications..."

Lupist looked out the window.

"Former King Adolf XII wasn't a saint, but he was practical. He even accepted that madman Gaold as the president of the association."

That explained everything.

"He had three sons. Their personalities are different, but none of them are practical. That means they are far from being suited for war. Expecting character from humans is foolish, but actions that are outwardly visible are a problem. There could be disturbances."

Shirone also knew all about the history before he was born.

'Still, I should see for myself. Their personalities might have changed with age.'

Having made his decision, Shirone nodded.

"Then I'll go."

"Remember, concepts like benevolence or Messiah won't work on them. To catch them early..."

Shirone tapped his inner pocket.

"Yes, I'll use this."

No royal could ignore the authority of the Ivory Tower's Integrated Universe Management Department.
