The chairman of the Ohsung Group, Lee Woon-sung, was once only well-known within his own country, and just an ordinary businessman overseas. However, now, there was no one on Earth who could ignore him.

No matter which country he visited, he would be treated as a state guest.

Of course, there was no real reason for him to travel abroad. It was far more worthwhile to travel through the beautiful Arsilan.

“Another refreshing day.”

He disconnected from the Soul Link and stepped out of the capsule.

The device he was using was the Soul Link Pro 2.

Soul Chips were being mass-produced, and the results were soon to be announced by Ohsung Electronics. The Soul Link Pro 2 would be released alongside the announcement.

Whereas before, the device had to be linked with a personal computer, the next-generation Soul Link came equipped independently with hardware developed by his grandson, Lee Jinwoo.

Everything could be handled comfortably within Arsilan Saga. It could even be linked with Ohsung Electronics’ smartphones to make regular and video calls.

‘Hmm, this is good.’

That was Lee Woon-sung’s direct feedback to his grandson Jinwoo.

‘Still, I feel like it could do even more.’

Jinwoo only showed things little by little, as if hiding the full extent of the technology. Only after persistent requests would he sigh and finally oblige.

He was like a magician concealing his power.

‘Shower function, automatic supplement injection… ‘

Lee Woon-sung carefully wrote out a feedback list to give to Jinwoo!

Then he stepped out of his room and headed to the kitchen. When he opened the fridge, it was packed with all sorts of healthy ingredients.

Tap tap tap!

Lee Woon-sung began cooking himself.

He couldn’t entrust his grandson’s meals to someone else! Strangely enough, his knife skills improved day by day. It was as if watching a master chef.

Once he finished cooking with satisfaction, Lee Hee-jin and Lee Cheon-woo approached, as if they had been waiting, and moved the dishes to the dining room. Then they sat down silently, observing each other’s expressions.

“What’s not going well?”

“No, everything is proceeding smoothly.”

“Haha, good. Do your best. Not that it’ll make things any easier.”

Lee Hee-jin’s eyebrow twitched.

‘We’re short on talent.’

Her guild members were each worth a hundred men. But because they were an elite few, they were short on manpower. It wasn’t her intention to keep the numbers low, it was just that most people couldn’t keep up with the concept. No, rather than a concept, her guild members called it their true nature.

Kids these days lacked wildness and instinct!

‘Hmm… ‘

Lee Hee-jin considered whether, once the Soul Link was mass-produced, she might “bring in”- no, “recruit”, some idols or actors from the entertainment agency she managed.

When Jinwoo came out, everyone looked at him and smiled brightly. Jinwoo flinched slightly and quietly took a seat.

“Looks like you’ve been busy lately.”

“Yes, well…”

“Haha! You’re preparing for a big event, so of course. I’m looking forward to it.”

When Lee Woon-sung said that, Lee Hee-jin also smiled brightly at Jinwoo, and Lee Cheon-woo patted him on the shoulder and gave him a nod.

All of these gestures were pressuring Jinwoo.

“Haha! Don’t go too far. If you make something even more incredible, the world will completely flip upside down.”

As always, the Ohsung family’s dining table was peaceful.

“Ahem, it seems like there’s a lack of musical instruments……”

“I saw on the Arsilan Worlds suggestion board…”

Amid such idle chatter, Jinwoo quietly focused on his meal.

“Hahaha! Keep up the good work today too.”

“Fufufu! Noona is cheering for you.”

“Haha! That’s my little brother!”

Lee Woon-sung, along with his granddaughter and grandson, saw Jinwoo off to the front of the mansion. They waved with smiles, but as soon as the car carrying Jinwoo disappeared, their smiles vanished completely. Then, without a word, each returned to their own duties.

Back in his room, Lee Woon-sung immediately received a report. It wasn’t a report about the Ohsung Group. It was a collection of reactions to Arsilan Saga.

One of the pleasures of starting his morning.
