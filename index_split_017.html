Chapter 67
Great Achievement (4)

Doubt everything!

As clues for the Village Opening Quest began to surface one by one, such symptoms grew even worse. This was because minor things that were easily overlooked were leading to chain quests.

‘I explored a cave and found a journal!’

‘I discovered a hidden passage in the corner of the gate!’

‘After learning D-rank detection magic, a path appeared in the fog!’

‘There’s now a new area accessible only to those level 40 and above?’

Such breaking news kept being posted on Arsilan Worlds. Although the quests were so deep and vast that they hadn’t yet led to an actual village opening, the series of updates boosted the spirits of the heroes.

[Planting 100 trees wasn’t a village opening quest. However, it rewards the rare alchemy material ‘Thick Blue Liquid’, so if you’re interested, feel free to try it out. There are continuous updates coming in right now. Currently attempting……]

Arsilan Broadcasting Station continuously reported such breaking news via their broadcasts. Thanks to the news from the now large-staffed Arsilan Broadcasting Station, heroes could receive updates more quickly. Since it was broadcast in radio mode, just listening to it was beneficial.

As clues for village opening were found, the atmosphere reached its peak. Amid that atmosphere, Jinwoo executed a specially prepared Village Opening Quest.

It wasn’t a simple village opening quest. There were multiple objectives.

‘Orcs are a race of war and battle.’

Orcs had very rich experience in war and combat.

When Jinwoo snapped his fingers, selected orcs appeared before, Evanth, and Marogo. He had informed them generally of what they needed to do, but since they lacked confidence in acting, he told them to just act naturally as they always would. Even if mistakes happened, Jinwoo could directly cover for them, so it was fine.

Jinwoo remained hidden and quietly observed.

The orcs, completely covered in thick leather robes, stood before each city.

Kunka, Bakal’s right-hand man, and the elite orcs appeared before Evanth with overwhelming presence. Jinwoo slightly altered their appearances. He combined some illusion magic and Tiris’s puppetry to give them the look of elderly orcs.

“Oh, orcs?”

“It’s the first time I’ve seen an orc who’s not a hero.”

“Wow… their aura is insane.”

Among the races available for selection in the Customizing Room, orcs were included, but they were the least popular. This was even more so since they hadn’t been seen in Arsilan. They gave off a strong impression of being a rootless, drifting race.

“Heroes! Battle and war! The day to spill blood on the battlefield approaches!”

Kunka’s shout shook the surroundings. His voice echoed off Evanth’s walls and spread even more fiercely throughout the area.

“We need brave warriors! Where are the great warriors who will become steel to sweep the battlefield and bless Arsilan with enemy blood!”

The heroes were overwhelmed by Kunka’s spirit.

“Please, prove your strength before me!”

Kunka and the orcs looked at the heroes. Kunka believed the heroes wouldn’t dare challenge him. Though the heroes did show the appearance of warriors, they still looked immature.

He thought the subjugation of the Erosion Core was mostly the work of the Immortal Archmage.

Kunka and the orcs believed so.

“Oh, oh! A quest?”

“This is crazy!”

“At this level, it must be an incredibly important quest!”

Contrary to Kunka’s thoughts, heroes began to gather around.

“Me! I’ll give it a shot!”

One hero stood before him with an innocent smile. At the same time, a window popped up stating that a duel had begun.

The hero grasped his weapon and charged forward.

He was about as skilled as an elite soldier of Arsilan, but to Kunka, he looked like a child.

Kunka didn’t even draw his dual swords, just watched the approaching hero and then smashed a fist into his face.

Bang!

The hero’s neck snapped, and he flew backward, tumbling roughly across the ground. Kunka had deliberately held back a great deal. Though it was said that heroes were free from death, a gap this large would crush their morale.

Since it was a duel, the hero whose face had been smashed and neck broken immediately revived. Kunka made eye contact with that hero. The hero had a dazed expression.

‘Hmm…, was that too much?’

That thought crossed his mind when.

“Uwaaah! He’s fucking strong! So damn cool! Kyaa! This is what an orc warrior should be like!”

Far from being discouraged, they were actually thrilled. The surrounding heroes were the same.

“A high-level quest popped up!”

“Sniffle, O Creator God! You haven’t forgotten the vanguard squad!”

“This is it! This is what I wanted!”

“Mm! My blood is boiling! A great battle! A holy war!”

Kunka and the orcs couldn’t help but be surprised. Like oil poured onto fire, their spirits flared even more fiercely. Challengers began lining up in front of the orcs.

The orcs consecutively destroyed the heroes who came at them.

And the more they did, the more the heroes’ eyes sparkled.

“Oh! He survived two hits this time.”

“He’s the tanker of our guild. Kyaa!”

“Orc hyung-nims, your movements are insane. Wow……”

The heroes who came to watch cheered, laughed, and shouted every time a challenger got crushed.

‘Amazing. Truly amazing.’

It was a matter that went beyond sheer strength.

Heroes craved battle even more than orcs. They could glimpse the appearance of berserkers who would drink enemy blood and bite hearts.

It was a madness enough to send chills.

They felt like beings born solely for combat.

With a savage smile, Kunka clenched his fist. Seeing the heroes like that made him feel exhilarated.

Heroes below level 40 couldn’t even attempt the challenge.

[Your weak body is overwhelmed by the orc’s spirit.]

[The blessing of Ella protects you. Grow a bit more before you challenge.]

Such messages popped up and prevented even approaching the area.

Heroes who had reached level 40 were extremely few.

Many named heroes took on the challenge, but Kunka defeated all of them without even drawing his weapon. As night fell, particles of light began to flow from Kunka’s body.

“Heroes! I shall return every day until one with the true qualifications of a warrior appears.”

Kunka and the orcs’ bodies faded and then disappeared completely.

“Kyaa!”

“This is so fun.”

“I want to fight like that too.”

Watching a named hero get brutally executed flooded them with dopamine. Moreover, the orc’s movements looked incredibly cool. They were so fast, they left afterimages, and the scene was more thrilling than any action scene in a movie. Every battle they’d experienced so far felt like child’s play in comparison.

Jinwoo teleported with the orcs to the place that would become the orc village. The orcs were still excited.

“How was it? Awesome, right?”

Jinwoo asked with a smile, and the orcs laughed heartily and nodded.

“It was magnificent. They love battle to a frightening degree. If Ella had seen it, she might’ve bestowed the crest of a berserker. We’ll have to face them with sincerity now.”

Even though it was a quest, Kunka and the orcs became serious. That alone was enough. Jinwoo was looking forward to what kind of spectacle the heroes would put on.

* * *

A quest for only the greatest of heroes, not just anyone.

It was the hottest topic in Arsilan Worlds. The orcs appeared with light particles at dawn in Arsilan. And when the sun set, they disappeared in particles of light.

Named heroes who surpassed level 40 continued to challenge them.

While others were busy chasing hidden quests, Northern Grand Duke focused on leveling up. Searching for things wasn’t her style. Her guild was the same.

They pursued only the ultimate strength, focusing every day on leveling up and researching new skills.

The orc quest was a blessing for her.

“Haha!”

Kunka’s fist grazed Northern Grand Duke’s cheek.

She met death from the punches that followed, but she was overjoyed.

She felt like she might go insane from the overwhelming joy.

She continued to challenge him. During the day, she took on the orc quest, and at night, she recalled the orc’s movements she had seen and trained, pouring her efforts into leveling up.

She had already taken an extended leave of absence. Her agency had also achieved outstanding results, so there was no one with the authority to stop her actions.

Even if someone had tried, she would have easily overpowered them.

Clang!

Northern Grand Duke’s two-handed axe was blocked by Kunka’s two-handed sword.

“You’ve finally drawn your weapons!”

It was the first time Kunka had wielded his weapons.

Northern Grand Duke let out a savage laugh, like a beast growling.

After that, she challenged him countless times and was defeated just as often.

When one arm was severed, she swung her axe with the other. When she could no longer use either arm, she charged in to bite. Seeing her like that, Kunka grinned with satisfaction and launched his attacks. One day passed, then several more.

The image of her struggling to endure a single blow vanished, and she was now able to last considerably longer. Kunka’s movements were visible in Northern Grand Duke’s form. Through actual combat, she had naturally mastered Kunka’s movements and techniques.

Kunka struck Northern Grand Duke with all his strength, then nodded.

“Excellent.”

Kunka raised his two-handed sword.

A massive current burst from the swords, and a blue glow surrounded them. When he brought them down before Northern Grand Duke.

Kwaaaaah!

The ground shattered, and fragments flew in all directions.

Northern Grand Duke’s gaze wavered. She had tried something similar before, but it was difficult to reproduce with skill combinations alone.

Perhaps sensing her thoughts, Kunka nodded and spoke.

“You have sufficient qualifications. All that’s needed is a suitable opportunity. The time will come.”

“Hmm……”

Kunka approached Northern Grand Duke and placed his hand on her shoulder. Then, a mark appeared on her body. The mark briefly shone, then faded away.

The heroes who had been watching cheered as they looked at her. The first hero to prove their qualification had appeared. The orcs, excluding Kunka, were still testing other heroes.

“Follow me.”

Northern Grand Duke walked after Kunka.

The two figures shimmered in light and disappeared from the spot.

“This place is……”

Northern Grand Duke was greatly surprised at the sight before her. A massive coliseum came into view. Surrounding the coliseum, buildings were lined up, and statues of orcs filled the area.

“They’re not statues.”

Northern Grand Duke instinctively felt that.

“It’s the curse of Erosion. A punishment for avoiding battle in fear of being corrupted by Erosion.”

“Punishment……”

“Yes, we are sinners. Sinners who turned our backs on Arsilan and fled. And this is what we’ve become.”

Kunka let out a deep sigh. Particles of light flowed from his body.

“Will you not break this curse and give our clan a chance at redemption? Only a great warrior who has proven their qualifications can release us.”

A quest window appeared before Northern Grand Duke.

[Orc] Path of Liberation

Enter the coliseum and awaken the great chief at the center of the curse, and lift the petrification curse. Those who have earned the qualification may challenge it with their guild, party, or companions. If you accept, all of Arsilan will cheer for you.

Reward: Orc Village unlocked, ???

Duration: Unlimited

Northern Grand Duke nodded.

[You have accepted the quest.]

Then, the Flowers of the Heroes appeared in front of the coliseum, and all of the guild members she designated were summoned.

“Oh, ohhh!”

“You did it!”

“Kyaa! As expected of the number one in combat power.”

Her guild members rejoiced and celebrated.

Northern Grand Duke looked at Kunka. Kunka’s body was gradually fading as it emitted particles of light.

“You are……”

“I’m already dead. Only my guilt is holding my soul together.”

Northern Grand Duke met Kunka’s eyes. The X-shaped scar covering his face was vividly clear. Kunka touched his scar with a bitter expression.

“It is the mark of a sinner who has no right to stand on the battlefield. The one before your eyes is no warrior, but merely a sinner.”

Northern Grand Duke remained silent for a moment.

She had learned so much from this old orc. She had learned how to move her body, how to harmonize her warrior skills with her own movements.

She had come to understand what battle truly meant.

He was the first teacher she had ever met in her life, having grown solely through her own power until now.

When Northern Grand Duke raised her hand, her guild members gathered around her. She drew a dagger and slashed her palm.

Splatter!

Blood poured onto the ground.

With her bloodied hand, she drew an X on her face. Her guild members followed her example.

“If this is the mark of a sinner, I will turn it into the symbol of a great warrior.”

At Northern Grand Duke’s words, Kunka’s eyes wavered.

“I will achieve a great feat on the battlefield and make the enemy tremble at this mark.”

Kunka’s body quivered slightly.

“I will make all the warriors of the world look up to it. I will make it the true symbol of a warrior that anyone would envy. So don’t worry, and rest in peace.”

Kunka engraved her image in his eyes.

The mark of a sinner had, through her, become a symbol of valor.

“Hero.”

Kunka bowed his head to her.

“I think I finally understand how the Lord of Erosion fell at your hands. You truly are a hero. I’m glad it was you who inherited my crest.”

Thud!

Kunka struck his chest with his fist.

“Hero. When the time comes, unleash everything. The potential you carry far surpasses what you think you have.”

With a gentle smile uncharacteristic of an orc, Kunka looked at her. Then, as if satisfied, he closed his eyes and turned into particles of light, disappearing.

Northern Grand Duke and her guild members bowed their heads briefly in a silent tribute for him.

“Let’s go.”

As she turned around and headed for the coliseum, her guild members smirked and slung their weapons over their shoulders. Then they followed behind her.

Upon entering the entrance of the coliseum, they were automatically transported to its center.

Like a Roman coliseum, seating surrounded the arena. In the seats stood numerous statues of orcs, with empty spots between them. At the center of the arena stood the enormous, statue of Great Chief orc.

With stone clinging all over its body, it looked almost like a golem.

[Your connections come to the coliseum.]

[This great battle will be broadcast from the Flowers of the Heroes in Evanth and Marogo.]

People of Arsilan appeared and filled the empty seats.

“Kyaaa! Unnie!”

“Waaaah! So cool! Feel the power of Northern Grand Duke!”

“Northern Grand Duke is invincible!”

“Everyone, hang in there!”

It was Naria, Lonta, and the guards from Gron Village.

“Hang in there!”

Hans raised his clenched fist and cheered for Northern Grand Duke. She chuckled faintly at the cheers. Her guild members did the same.

Everyone was watching.

There was absolutely no room for defeat.

[The battle begins!]

The petrified Great Chief began to move. Compared to Kunka, its movements were slow.

Shhhhk!

A dark stream flowed from the stones clinging to its body. It appeared the stones contaminated by Erosion seemed to be making the great chief move.

Northern Grand Duke and her guild members rushed in.

When the chief swung his fist, several guild members were flung away and crashed into the walls.

The power was immense.

“Haaah!”

Northern Grand Duke closed in and swung her two-handed axe with all her might. The speed was so great it left afterimages, just like Kunka’s movements. The force behind her two-handed axe was formidable.

However.

Ting!

She couldn’t penetrate the stones encasing the chief’s body. They were harder than steel, there wasn’t even a scratch.

“Ugh!”

She rolled sideways to dodge the chief’s heavy punch.

“This is insane!”

“Noona! Our attacks aren’t working!”

“And it’s so damn fast too! Guh!”

Guild members flew in every direction, crashing and rolling across the ground. One guild member’s body was shattered and turned to ash. Northern Grand Duke kept approaching and unleashing skills and attacks, but she couldn’t destroy the stone.

Boom!

When she blocked a approaching punch with her two-handed axe, her body was pushed far back.

“Haaah……”

Seeing her guild members die filled her with boiling rage.

Her body was burning hot.

From the seats, her friends were cheering with all their might. She absolutely couldn’t fall here.

Northern Grand Duke threw herself into the battle with all her strength, continuously attacking the Great Chief.

Boom!

She was struck in the abdomen by one of the chief’s blows and rolled across the ground.

Kugh!”

Using her two-handed axe as a staff, she pushed herself up. Her vision blurred and cleared repeatedly.

The battle had gone on for a long time.

Northern Grand Duke and her guild members had fallen many times and rolled across the ground countless times.

‘It’s manageable.’

Yet strangely, it didn’t feel exhausting.

‘I can still move.’

The desperate cheers, the cries of her guild members, and the roaring noise from the Great Chief, all of it felt like it was accumulating inside her.

She wanted to let it all out.

She wanted to release everything with all her might. Northern Grand Duke threw off her outer garment. Her leather armor only covered part of her chest and abdomen.

‘Unleash everything.’

The final words of the old orc came to her mind. Northern Grand Duke took a deep breath. She drove her two-handed axe into the ground and clenched her fists.

Her mouth opened.

A tremendous roar exploded from her body, shaking the coliseum. It went beyond the level of a mere shout and stirred the bodies of all present.

Sssshk!

Her skin began to glow as if it were burning, and a symbol was drawn upon her.

The roar of the battlefield.

A war cry resembling that of the orcs.

It elevated everything within her and her guild members.

“Valhalla!”

“Graaaah! Valhalla!”

“Uwaaaah!”

Her guild members, seized by madness, charged at the Great Chief. She too let out a massive roar and raised her two-handed axe high.

What the old orc had shown.

She now understood what to do.

The time had come.

She left no strength in reserve to dodge.

With all her might, she charged toward the Great Chief. She poured all her power into her two-handed axe.

Fwaaash!

A current burst from her two-handed axe, glowing red. The symbol of a warrior beyond expectation finally revealed itself.

Her two-handed axe grazed the heavy stone fist and drove into the Great Chief’s chest.

KWAANG!

The Great Chief staggered back a few steps from the impact. The stones shattered and clattered to the ground. At the same time, the statues in the seats began to crack and break.

The stones that had bound the Great Chief all disappeared, revealing his true form.

Having expended all her strength, Northern Grand Duke fell to her knees.

The Great Chief walked toward her. He reached out and helped her to her feet.

To anyone watching, it was clearly her victory.

“Orcs! Rejoice in the birth of a great warrior! A great warrior has freed us and paved the way for redemption!”

The Great Chief cried out in joy.

Crack! KWAANG!

The statues in the seats all shattered, revealing the orcs within.

“Shout loudly for the great warrior!”

“Kraaaaah!”

The orcs beat their chests to make a drum-like sound and shouted. Hans and the people of Arsilan stood up and cheered with joy.

[You have liberated the orcs from the Curse of Petrification.]

[Please name the village and the arena.]

“Northern Village…, Valhalla!”

Northern Grand Duke gave the name without hesitation.

[‘Path of Liberation’ quest has been completed.]

[Northern Village has been unlocked.]

[The battlefield of warriors, Valhalla, has been unlocked!]

*A statue of you will be erected in Valhalla.

*Valhalla will now be open to all heroes. You can engage in battle as 1:1, party, or guild units.

*All orcs recognize you and your guild members as comrades.

*All heroes may use Northern Village Contribution Points to learn orc combat techniques. However, you and your guild members may learn all techniques without using Contribution Points.

The Orc Village was unlocked.

Coincidentally, the Orc Village was located at the far north.

Named heroes who had just passed their trials and reached the village smiled with bittersweet regret but applauded and congratulated her.

Great Chief Bakal looked toward the Archmage sitting in the stands.

When the Archmage gave a thumbs-up, Bakal nodded and grabbed Northern Grand Duke’s arm.

Woosh!

Bakal raised her arm high.

“The Orc Village is now the Northern Village! This place where everyone’s blood and flesh are scattered is Valhalla!”

“Northern! Northern!”

“Valhalla! Valhalla!”

At Bakal’s shout, all the orcs roared.

The orcs imprinted the image of Northern Grand Duke in their eyes. The X-shaped symbol on her face, the same as theirs yet entirely different in meaning, deeply resonated within their hearts.

Perhaps, as she said, this mark of a sinner might truly transform into one of glory.

It was the moment a great achievement was born.
