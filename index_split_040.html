Once they passed level 40, they could at least go to the desert even if only as odd-job workers, so everyone was putting in the effort to reach the minimum level requirement.

As the preparations for the invasion progressed, the civilization of Arsilan was developing rapidly. The demand for goods needed for the invasion was skyrocketing, and many inventions were pouring out.

“Selling fans powered by magic! They have freezing spells on them and are cooler than air conditioners!”

Even the Heroes who had previously saved their money did not hesitate to open their wallets. The economy of Arsilan was soaring rapidly!

A large amount of money was needed for the invasion.

Ships had to be built, and exclusive equipment for the invasion had to be custom-made. Rather than the heavy armor that Heroes had worn until now, light gear that allowed nimble movement was preferred. And food was necessary too. Although Heroes’ bodies could be sustained with just magic power, hunger still occurred, and if they starved, they would receive debuffs.

If the expedition team was small, costs could be somewhat reduced, but for large-scale expeditions, the costs rose significantly. Even guilds couldn’t handle it on their own.

“We are recruiting investors for the Ragnar Expedition Team! It’s an expedition formed by selecting 300 elite Heroes! We disclose all information transparently, so please check us out!”

“Looking for sponsors to grow together!”

“There will be an investment presentation today at 7 PM! Everyone attending will receive a small gift!”

Because of that, they recruited investors like this.

FS Games immediately created an investment system.

The treasures looted by expedition teams would be distributed to investors according to the amount they had invested, or sold and the principal and profits would be shared. It was effectively the appearance of a corporation in Arsilan.

Expedition teams centered around named Heroes attracted many investors. Heroes who had accumulated wealth through production jobs actively started investing.

Many investors flocked to the ‘Patriot Company’ that Sillion Kingdom had created with big money. If an expedition team was registered with the Patriot Company, investors could review the information and decide to invest.

“Oh… it’s a new expedition team, but it looks like the returns will be pretty good.”

“That’s what investment is! I’m going all in with my entire fortune! Let’s go to the moon!”

He had some leisure time!

‘Then what should I do?’

Should I take a vacation and go on a trip?

Watch a movie? Or…

He used to imagine such happy thoughts while buried in work hell, but now that he actually had the time, something felt off. He wanted to do something with enthusiasm, but his body wouldn’t move, he was in that kind of state.

Jinwoo stepped out of the CEO’s office and looked around the office.

The office was completely empty, as everyone seemed to have already left. While walking down the hallway, he saw some employees heading somewhere. Their faces were full of excitement.

They were headed toward the company’s gym.

Jinwoo followed them inside. Inside, not just the team leaders but even the new employees were all gathered.

It seemed they had gathered as soon as work ended.

“Shouldn’t we pool all our money together to increase the size first?”

“That would be better, but we don’t have enough funds.”

“How about we bring in the materials ourselves and build it? Even if it takes time, that might be better.”

They were seriously discussing with one another. Even the new employees were voicing their opinions without hesitation.

Jinwoo tilted his head in curiosity and approached them. No one noticed he had come closer, likely because they were so focused.

“Aren’t you all going home?”

“Gasp! CEO-nim!?”

Only after Jinwoo spoke did the employees jump in surprise and look at him. It looked like they’d been caught plotting something in secret.

“Well…”

“This is our regular meeting day.”

The employees were talking about Arsilan Saga. Looking closely, they were even wearing matching t-shirts.

‘God’s Expedition Team?’

That phrase was printed on them.

Since they were FS Games employees, there were some restrictions in the game, but they could still enjoy Arsilan Saga.

Inside the company building was a Soul Link gaming room with state-of-the-art facilities. It looked like they planned to log into Arsilan Saga there instead of going home.

“Bwahaha! The time of great heroes has come…! CEO-nim!?”

“Why so surprised? It’s not illegal to play games.”

“I’m fine. Don’t worry about me and have fun.”

They all looked happy.

God’s Expedition Team.

“I, I see.”

“Please stop in front of that chicken place for a moment.”

“I’ll go in and get it.”

The interior was dark. He turned on the lights and checked the living room and dining room, but couldn’t sense anyone around. It seemed like no one was home yet.

“Should I wait a little?”

For the first time, Jinwoo waited for his family at home!

He passed the time watching various videos uploaded on Arsilan Worlds, waiting for the chicken to cool down, but no one came home.

Even though they could take calls in Arsilan Saga when linked with Soul Link, if call reception was blocked, they would get that kind of message. Not only his grandfather, sister, and hyung, but even his father had that same message.

This is rest, right?

This is…

“Ah! I’m on my break right now.”

The Empire of Evil was vast. So vast that the Kingdom of Sillion looked tiny in comparison.

Within this massive land, lords ruled over their own territories and reigned like kings. These were the high nobles, the ruling class of the vast Empire of Evil.

He felt like dropping a meteor on them, but he didn’t. The perfect revenge against the Lord of Greed was to take away all the fruits of his greed.

If all the treasures were looted, he wouldn’t be able to accumulate more greed. Besides, everything here was content for the Heroes to enjoy. The many things in this empire would entertain the Heroes and help them grow stronger.

‘Hmm? It feels more chaotic now.’

“That land is mine! Hand it over as promised!”

“Father! There’s nothing more to hear! He’s just holding out after taking the gold we gave him!”

These bloated individuals were gathered, discussing all sorts of matters.

It seemed that the Lord of Greed was intervening and issuing rulings to ensure the smooth extraction of greed. It was clear they were running things in a way that would maximize greed and suffering.

However, the Lord of Greed could no longer control them. He was still feeding off their greed, but the damage he had suffered was so great he could no longer communicate his will. That’s why all these conflicts were arising.

‘So that’s why it felt chaotic.’

To Jinwoo, the whole situation was just amusing.

“Why don’t we continue the discussion after a meal? I’ve prepared some fine dishes.”

It was worth trying.
