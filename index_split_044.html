Jayron du Balac.

‘How could God still not respond?’

Occasionally, beings from the outside would cross over like that invader, but God had always revealed His authority and destroyed them. The Count assumed this invader was the same kind.

Even though God wasn’t answering now, the contract bearing His name should still wield absolute power. Besides, he had entrusted the city to Kumel, so he thought there would be no problem.

The Count, who had been leading a large force, turned back upon hearing the unbelievable news.

How could anyone commit such a cruel act!
