MagicMark Street No. 1, Blingjena Perimeter Road, SmokingGirl Three-Way Intersection, and so on, the treatment they received was undeniable.

Honor!

It could not be compared to expensive items.

No matter how good the item you possessed now, eventually, better items would be released. Then, it was only natural for them to be discarded and forgotten.

However, if you could name a city or village and engrave your name there forever? You would be permanently recorded in Arsilan Saga.

What greater honor could there be?

As it was, the vanguard Heroes were already very interested in achievement farming.

They would be able to add one truly magnificent line to introduce themselves.

Everyone’s spirit was fired up.

There must be places that hadn’t been opened yet.

So let’s hurry up and find them!

All the Heroes began to assign meaning even to trivial things. Following wandering NPCs or analyzing every single item dropped by monsters became part of the daily routine.

Heroes who obsessed over the smallest things began to appear.

“Hmm, this looks strange? Why is only this one lying sideways?”

There were many who spent hours studying the various shapes of rocks in the fields or lingering around unique trees or natural environments.

“Hmm, this feels off. There’s definitely something here.”

There was one white tooth among the ogre’s teeth that was the only one not decayed, and one Hero, suspicious of that, pulled it out and collected it.

“What, something’s happening! Ooh!”

In truth, it wasn’t a city-opening quest, but it was a hidden quest. If you collected two hundred of those teeth, the Tooth Fairy would appear and give you a Gold Tooth Skin. When worn, all your front teeth turned to gold.

“Ah.”

The face of the Hero who received the Gold Tooth Skin was filled with disappointment.

After wearing it, they launched the mirror app and looked at their own teeth.

“Oh… this is actually kind of nice in its own way.”

The sparkling gold teeth looked quite unique!

The Hero who collected the ogre teeth was satisfied in his own way.

“Wow! That’s amazing. What is that?”

“I got it by pulling teeth from two hundred ogres.”

“Crazy… you totally deserve that.”

The Hero proudly showed it off everywhere.

Jinwoo nodded as he watched the whole scene.

There were still too many hidden quests left undiscovered. Many quests had been created to meet the staff’s work quotas, and even now, they continued to increase. When the developers at FS Games came up with a plan, it was immediately implemented, and even that wasn’t enough, Jinwoo himself planned them directly.

Wouldn’t it be fine, as long as everyone was happy?

Jinwoo moved to the skies above Marogo and looked down at the city’s view. All essential NPCs had been placed, and the city was running normally. The setting was that the city operated under Ella’s authority and wisdom.

International City Marogo was governed by loose rules and was far freer than Evanth.

“The city achievement quests have also been properly applied.”

City achievement quests were added not only to Evanth but also to Marogo. They were quests that could only be cleared when all Heroes joined forces. They were also connected to the main quests.

Slowly but surely, everything in Arsilan was becoming unified.

A bit of magic had been cast to help users quickly learn languages.

The number of connections had already far surpassed one million.

Jinwoo could feel the Soul Gate becoming stronger.

“Phew.”

He closed his eyes for a moment and thought about what he desired most right now.

[Transcendent] Soul Gate +2

A heart born of pure will tempers the soul.

The more Heroes are summoned and the more they grow, the stronger the Soul Gate becomes, and accordingly, its authority also improves.

*You can now borrow the power of a Hero to completely subdue and enslave a spiritual being.

*You can now carry more objects.

*Souls that die in Arsilan now head only to the Soul Gate and are not consumed by Erosion.

After confirming the information, a smile appeared on Jinwoo’s lips.

The Arsilanians could not cross into the domain of Erosion, but now, at least within Arsilan, they could withstand it. That alone removed a great number of penalties.

What Jinwoo had hoped for.

It was for the beings of Arsilan to regain their ancient powers.

That hope and possibility were made real by the Heroes.

Now, the Arsilanians could also become a source of strength for the Heroes.

Just as the Heroes gave their all, so too must the Arsilanians.

“Now…”

Now it was their time to move forward.

Jinwoo passed beyond his territory and headed to the far side.

The last Arsilan.

It was a land as vast as Rainsword, but it was an utterly desolate land. Among it, there was an especially barren area, like a desert where not a single blade of grass grew.

There, lived the race Jinwoo was looking for.

They had not even formed a barrier, instead turning their own bodies into stone to prepare for the end.

Jinwoo descended to the earth.

So dry was it that the moment he stepped on it, everything crumbled into dust and scattered. It was a land where not even a drop of water could be found.

Jinwoo looked around.

The land was filled with statues.

Each statue was massive. They were much larger and more heavily built than Jinwoo, with muscles that looked ready to burst. But they hung their heads low and curled up in submission, looking terribly pitiful.

All of their faces bore an X-shaped scar. They had carved them themselves.

Orcs.

A race that worshipped combat.

They considered dying on the battlefield the highest honor, and Ella, in recognition of their valor, had bestowed markings of bravery upon their bodies. The orcs turned to stone were all covering those markings.

Jinwoo passed through the many statues and headed toward the center. There stood Bakal, the Great Chief of the orcs, who was braver than any. His body bore countless scars, but the deepest one was the scar he had carved into his own face.

“We really were too late.”

Once one fully turned to stone, even if the soul became corrupted, they would not move. The soul might suffer, but they would not endure the disgrace of becoming a slave.

That was the path chosen by the orcs.

One hundred years.

More than enough time to become fully petrified. Most likely, they had all turned to stone decades ago. If so, even magic could not return them.

“I wish you would help us.”

The stage where they could give everything of themselves was approaching. Without them, the atmosphere of battle would never reach its climax.

With that intention in his heart, Jinwoo cast a de-petrification spell on Bakal. So much time had passed that it may well have been a meaningless act.

Crack!

Stone dust crumbled from Bakal’s surface.

Crackle!

As the surface began to split, the stones started to fall away. As Bakal moved, all the stone encasing his body tumbled to the ground.

Bakal’s true form was revealed.

Bakal straightened his once-bent body. A wild and overwhelming aura surged from him, like beholding a massive boss monster. The power that once shattered mountains still remained within him.

Bakal stepped forward, staring at Jinwoo. Looking down at him, he clenched his fist. Jinwoo silently met his gaze.

Thud!

A heavy tremor rang out.

Bakal had knelt before him.

“I’m sorry.”

He apologized to Jinwoo.

“We fled in disgrace. We feared becoming corrupted and enslaved by Erosion. Consumed by despair and fear, we couldn’t fight by your side.”

These mythic beings who once boasted of greatness had fallen, and the kingdom of the orcs vanished in vain. Corrupted by Erosion, they lost their light and glory, becoming mere slaves.

When the Core of Erosion appeared, Bakal chose retreat over battle, leading his kin away.

A cowardly orc who ran from corruption.

Blaming his weakness for turning away from combat, he carved the X-shaped scar on his face.

Orcs considered a glorious death in battle the highest value. It would not be an exaggeration to say they lived for that alone. However, Erosion defiled and insulted that value.

Erosion, a corruption beyond death, had crushed them.

That despair and hopelessness left an unhealable scar upon the orcs.

The atmosphere was unbearably heavy.

Bakal deeply bowed his head.

“Your coming to me must mean the final moment is near. A chance for atonement… even if this soul becomes corrupted, I will fight alongside you until the very end.”

“Why didn’t you become fully petrified?”

“…I couldn’t do it.”

“Letting my comrades fight alone… that was a humiliation worse than corruption. I realized it far too late.”

“Well, even if you had said you’d fight with me, I probably would have turned you down. You’d just have been another hard opponent for me to protect.”

The Immortal Archmage.

Watching that made even Bakal feel uplifted.
