Could it be that she was going to give them a ride?

“Huh?”

At that moment.

Kwaaaa!

“Uaaaaaa!”

“Kyaaaak!”

Helby folded her wings and accelerated as if a rocket had launched. The air in front of her heated up and emitted a bright light. Countless heroes bound by chains were ascending alongside her.

At least once as a child, you might have imagined what it would feel like to be strapped to a rocket and fly.

That imagination had now become reality.

In an instant, the black ground grew distant.

The vanished Magic Barrier became visible, and the brilliant figure of Arsilan contrasted against the black land. The silhouette of Rainsword appeared briefly, but the scene behind it was blurred and obscured.

They pierced through the black clouds of the red sky.

Above the sky, countless starlights and a shattered moon had taken position.

“Wow!”

“Ah……”

The remaining Arsilan, the Lost Land once ruled by Erosion, was just a small portion compared to that vast planet. Other parts shimmered as if space itself had been sliced away, some were dyed black, and some were engulfed in massive typhoons. There were even regions that couldn’t be seen at all.

Haebyul was in a daze.

UruBear was the same. Hellhound and the Helby Lovers Group clenched their fists tightly.

Lands to adventure in.

A space where they could run wild freely. However, it could not be reached even from the sky, as though space had been severed.

Kwaaaa!

Helby did not reduce her speed and charged directly toward the black skull.

“Could it be, just like this…”

The continued attacks from the heroes caused the Core of Erosion unbearable pain. It screamed, begging for it to stop. Jinwoo did not stop.

[Uh, uhhh…, uh……]

Jinwoo, who had been controlling the black skull, realized that the Core of Erosion had reached its limit. The screams, which no one else could hear, gradually quieted down.

It was stuttering, as if it had malfunctioned.

Kwaduduk!

Cracks began to spread across the entire Core of Erosion.

It could no longer withstand the heroes’ attacks.

Clang!

The will of Erosion, which Jinwoo had forcibly held together, was shattering. At the same time, Erosion gradually began to disappear from the Lost Land.

Jinwoo did not simply let the Core of Erosion go. Chains flowing out from the Soul Gate coiled around the Core of Erosion. The completely subdued Core of Erosion was bound by the Soul Gate.

“There must be another way to use you.”

[Kyaaaaaa!]

With a final scream, the Core of Erosion completely collapsed.

How long had he waited for this moment?

It was exhilarating.

It was so satisfying that he couldn’t help but laugh. But a heaviness sank in one corner of his heart. There were too many precious things that could never be recovered by such revenge.

As the will within the core was sucked into the Soul Gate, a light burst out from the Core of Erosion.

It was a light Jinwoo hadn’t expected.

That light was incredibly warm.

‘This is… ‘

The light covered Jinwoo’s vision and wrapped around his body. The fragments of the collapsing black skull, even the completely shattered Core of Erosion, all froze in that moment.

It felt as if his consciousness had been pulled into another space.

Was it a space the Core of Erosion had occupied?

Was it a power triggered by the Soul Gate?

It was still unclear.

What spread out before his eyes was a white space.

A vast white space with no horizon in sight. Jinwoo stood there for a moment, gazing into the white expanse. Strangely, he felt at ease.

It felt like coming home.

He sensed presences behind him.

So many presences. Then came laughter he could never forget.

“………”

Jinwoo’s eyes trembled violently.

His fists clenched on their own. His breathing grew rough, and his heart pounded wildly. His vision blurred and cleared repeatedly from the intense beating.

Jinwoo let out a long breath and barely managed to calm his trembling body. Then, he slowly turned around.

His lips trembled.

He had imagined this moment more than a thousand times.

He had even practiced for it.

But now, he couldn’t think of a single thing to say as his mind went completely blank. Despite being a Archmage, his thoughts were paralyzed by emotion. The emotional control magic circle he had placed on his chest had long since shattered.

Standing before his eyes were countless people.

Now colorless, translucent souls, standing before him just like they once had.

He saw the merchant who had given him all the magic stone chunks. Without him, the Magic Barrier could never have been created.

There were the knight captain and knights who had held back the Erosion Legion until the Magic Barrier was erected. There were also the mages who had believed in his claim and devoted everything to the Magic Tower.

The king he had spent decades with had, in his final moments, become a massive wall to send only Jinwoo to the remaining land. Jinwoo had no choice but to erect the Magic Barrier while watching their backs.

Thud!

Jinwoo fell to his knees. His upper body collapsed forward.

“…I’m sorry.”

Those were the first words that came out.

It had taken far too long.

He had once tried to give up. Crushed by despair, he had tried to run away in hopeless surrender. That had become a big nail piercing his chest. He had reached this place after over a hundred years, using all the history they had left behind.

Perhaps, he had even dishonored their nobility.

“I’m truly sorry. I…”

Moisture gathered on the back of Jinwoo’s hand.

The Archmage, who had never once broken down over all those long years, collapsed here. He poured out every emotion he had held back and suppressed all this time.

Swiik!

“From the moment you brought light to the Land of Erosion, we were able to rest.”

They had endured the torture of Erosion without falling to corruption. So that when Jinwoo finally arrived on this land, they would not become his enemies.

Because of that, their souls had lost all color. They had faded into nothingness.

A hundred years.

It was too long.

It had taken so very long.

Because they had endured for too long, because they had suffered terrible torture, their souls were already at their limit.

“That is where we shall return. It looks more comfortable than even Ella’s embrace. Our souls will serve as good nourishment for Arsilan.”
