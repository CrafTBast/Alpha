Chapter 83
Episode 2 (3)

Jinwoo worked diligently, going back and forth between Arsilan and FS Games.

The promises he had made so liberally were truly vast.

From quick commitments like skill training grounds, power measurement device additions, guild battles, paid costumes, enhancement system expansion and improvement, hunting grounds for high-level Heroes, newbie support, trade item improvements, and more, he handled them swiftly.

Once improvement proposals passed at FS Games, Jinwoo personally went to Arsilan and added each one. Since most of the promises significantly impacted Arsilan Saga, he had no choice but to add them carefully.

The most difficult issue was the new races. It was the most complicated since it required creating a race not currently existing in Arsilan. They held quite a long meeting in the FS Games conference room.

“Creating an entirely higher race might not be a bad idea.”

That was Han Yong-seon’s opinion.

Once a character reached a certain level, it seemed reasonable to grant one good trait and allow them to ascend into a higher race. Trait granting was a high-level magic. It wasn’t the casting of the spell that was the problem, but the target. The soul needed to be strong enough to endure it.

They had to be at least level 100 to engrave it stably.

“That sounds like it could work.”

Since he could base it on the existing races of Arsilan, it wouldn’t feel too out of place.

“Then we’ll start planning various options!”

“For the Beastkin, maybe we could try adding the nine-tailed fox…”

“There have also been requests to unlock the Fairy race…”

Once a concrete direction was set, the discussion progressed rapidly.

The next agenda item followed. The Art Team began their presentation. On the holographic display, costumes designed by the Art Team appeared.

“These are swimsuits for the Grand Festival.”

From modest designs to daring ones, the variety was extensive. Numerous accessories suitable for swimwear also appeared in abundance.

“Oh.”

“I think the Heroes will love these!”

“These look like they’ll sell really well.”

The staff reactions were also positive.

Even the swimsuits planned to be given for free had decent designs.

‘Arsilan has become peaceful now… ‘

Before the Great War, this would never have been released, but now was a time where one could take off their armor and enjoy things.

“Well then…”

The Art Team leader’s eyes sparkled.

The staff were filled with anticipation. On the holographic screen, swimsuits worn by Leon, Helby, and various Arsilan characters appeared.

“Ooooh!”

“They’re beautiful. If I saw them in real life, I’d probably be stunned!”

“Whoa, Helby… so cute!”

They were hand-drawn illustrations.

The Art Team’s skill was phenomenal!

It wasn’t overly revealing. By strictly following the aesthetics of concealment, it actually sparked the imagination even more. The designs brought out each character’s charm. From the materials to the tiniest patterns, incredible dedication could be felt.

“Our team has been preparing this since before the Great War.”

“Oh, ooh!”

“As expected of the Art Team!”

Clap, clap, clap!

The staff gave a round of applause.

If this had been just a regular game, Jinwoo would have nodded in satisfaction.

‘I’ll have to try to persuade them.’

Jinwoo’s mind became complicated.

But now there was no turning back. He had already posted the announcement for the ‘Grand Festival’.

Swimsuits were essential attire that the people of Arsilan had to wear during the Grand Festival.

Having returned to Arsilan, Jinwoo first implemented all the costumes. The main characters of Arsilan Saga all had individual festival costumes and swimsuits designed, so they had to wear them.

Jinwoo summoned everyone and handed out the outfits.

“So this is the swimsuit?”

“Not bad.”

Tia and Tiris’s reactions weren’t bad. Perhaps because they were custom-designed, they seemed to like them. Although they took on slightly awkward postures due to the exposed stomach and legs, they enjoyed themselves while looking in the mirror.

Luceren held a bikini swimsuit in her hands and nodded.

“I’ve heard our ancestors used to cover their bodies with only leaves. During this festival period, it feels like returning to those times.”

Luceren was satisfied in a different sense. Jinwoo handed her all the outfits for the elves as well.

Heina, upon seeing her own outfit, let out a sigh of relief. It was a rash guard. Although her figure was still visible, she seemed satisfied that her stomach wasn’t exposed.

“Heina, have your knights maintain order during the Grand Festival.”

“I will follow your command!”

Hallon, Hasman, and the men including the Orcs smirked.

“We worked out just for this day.”

“We’ve overcome the Trial of Light. It’s time to show off my muscles.”

It seemed Hallon and Hasman had been exercising all this time. Kunka, who had become the Great Chief after Bakal, also grinned.

“It’s time to show what a warrior’s muscles truly are.”

Not just the Orcs, but knights and other retainers nodded in agreement.

“Um, what about me?”

Saintess Airi cautiously raised her hand.

Jinwoo was very conflicted. The swimsuit designed by the Art Team for her wasn’t a bikini but a one-piece. It was designed with her status as a Saintess in mind. But was it really appropriate to give her a swimsuit?

Even fully clothed, she had a horde of fanatics following her. If she wore a swimsuit…

Airi’s expression was serious.

During the Great War, she hadn’t been able to properly act as a Saintess. She only had the role in name. That’s why she wanted to do anything she could for the Hero and for Arsilan.

“Uh…, well……”

“I’ll do my best too!”

Jinwoo stopped speaking.

Airi had rarely ever volunteered herself. So he had no choice but to support her.

Jinwoo handed the swimsuit to Airi.

“Alright, I’ll be looking forward to it.”

Somehow, it would work out.

Well, having more Clerics in Arsilan wasn’t a bad thing.

Helby appeared last.

“Take it.”

“What is this?”

When Jinwoo handed over the swimsuit, she frowned.

“You want me to wear this piece of fabric?”

“If you don’t want to, there’s nothing I can do.”

“Hmph.”

Helby turned away and started walking off.

“Then, during the Grand Festival, go stay somewhere else.”

“What?”

“During the Grand Festival, everyone has to wear festival attire.”

Helby turned her head to look at Jinwoo.

Outside Solis, the Heroes were preparing for the Grand Festival. Jinwoo had heard that she frequently appeared there.

“Hmph, hmm!”

Helby’s body moved back toward Jinwoo.

At that moment, Airi came out wearing her swimsuit.

“Oh, um……”

“Mmm!”

“Amazing. She really must have the blood of the Succubus Queen.”

Everyone nodded while looking at Airi. Jinwoo grinned as he looked at her.

“Airi! During the festival, could you help out with the Helby Lovers Group and the Platinum Coin Shop?”

“Got it! I’ll do my best!”

At Jinwoo’s request, Airi clenched her fist and nodded firmly.

“At this rate, all the Helby Lovers Group kids are going to become Clerics.”

With Airi as she was now, Jinwoo’s words might really come true.

“You. Stay away.”

Helby gave a warning to Airi and then.

Whoosh!

Snatched the swimsuit and disappeared.

“Phew.”

He’d somehow persuaded her!

Jinwoo felt proud of himself.

“Airi, thank you.”

“Huh?”

Jinwoo expressed his gratitude to Airi.

Airi only tilted her head in confusion.
