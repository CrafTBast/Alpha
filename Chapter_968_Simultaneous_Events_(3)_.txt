Chapter 968: Simultaneous Events (3)

King Adolph XIII and the royals had emerged from the underground bunker.

The royal palace, with its ceiling collapsed, was severely unpleasant, but there was no time to grumble.

"What in the world has happened? A battle in the capital that even the king knows nothing about. Summon those involved in this matter immediately."

"But currently, prioritizing the situation’s control is..."

"Uncle." King Adolph XIII's eyes flashed with anger.

"Yes, Your Majesty."

"I am the king. What use is a state if royal authority is not restored, even in wartime?"

Though he was new to being king and his nervousness was understandable, that made it all the more challenging.

'Everyone has refused.'

That those of high direct lineage did not respond to the summons was proof of their lack of trust in King Adolph XIII.

Robins, the highest in direct lineage and Adolph XIII's twin, said,

"Your Majesty, the citizens already regard you as a great king. The politicians will follow the populace, and eventually, the staff of each institution will come around."

"Hmm."

It was, after all, nice to hear.

"Furthermore, the fact that the army of the Holy War is stationed in Vashka presents a great opportunity. If we make Thormia the central nation of the world war, we can receive substantial aid from other countries."

The royals agreed.

"Indeed. Especially, we should levy a tax on the southern continent. Aren’t we acting as their shield?"

King Adolph XIII nodded.

"I think the same. Speaking of which... I heard that a figure from the Ivory Tower is here."

"It's Shirone Arian from the Universal Management Bureau, one of the Five Stars. A native who is a beacon of benevolence and a major player in the world war."

"Hmph, benevolence."

King Adolph XIII did not believe in what he could not see.

"Regardless, treat him with the utmost respect. No matter the cost."

After all, with the recognition of the Ivory Tower, it could be covered by aid from other countries.

"Understood. But before that, there is one thing that needs to be resolved."

"Resolve?"

Robins narrowed his eyes as he spoke.

"127th in direct lineage, Pony."

"Cough."

Anger flared on King Adolph XIII's face.

"Currently, one of the Five Stars here and Pony have studied together at the magic school. If she persuades Shirone first..."

A royal shouted, chilled by the sinister thought.

"We must kill her! Didn't you see earlier? She cast magic without hesitation. She relinquished her royal rights merely to survive. Now that the city is stable, she will surely make a move."

King Adolph XIII stroked his chin.

"What can I possibly do about it? It's difficult enough to persuade a first-grade citizen of the Ivory Tower, let alone one of the Five Stars."

"Who knows, she might use her beauty."

It was a statement that undermined royal authority, but if the subject was Pony, it was a different story.

"Haven't we sensed something from before? She even participated in the World Beauty Contest to enter the royal direct lineage. She knows her beauty is exceptional and acts accordingly. She will not hesitate to use any means necessary."

The possibility of royal overthrow suddenly seemed realistic.

"… Can you do it? The Magic Association will not help us, and the military command has passed to the Holy War. If we don't kill her definitively, it could backfire on us."

Robins spoke.

"I have a plan. It involves using criminals."

"The prison? But isn’t the high-security prison for A-grade criminals and above managed by the Magic Association?"

"There is a suitable person in the Inferno prison in the west."

It was a card he had prepared for the eventual need to kill his father or brother, never imagining it would be used like this.

The royals were puzzled.

"Inferno? Even if it’s ruthless, it's only B-grade, right?"

"Although that's true..."

It was unnecessary to say that the person was not imprisoned for committing crimes.

"I can vouch for his skills. He’s the son of Gunjo Bedium, the world’s top hitman, and also a graduate of the same magic school as Shirone and Pony."

"Gunjo Bedium, the Black Line?" The name alone made the royals shiver.

'His son is in Inferno? No, before that, how does Robins even know him...'

For a moment, the royals' faces turned pale, but they soon regained their composure.

'Well, it's only natural.'

Those within the top ten of the royal direct lineage had the potential to become king and each held at least one card.

'Let’s not show it. Getting rid of Pony comes first.'

No danger was comparable to the crisis of her possibly persuading Shirone.

King Adolph XIII showed no change in expression.

He knew, of course, but he was the king.

"Good. The matter of Pony, Robins, I leave to you. But make sure it is dealt with decisively."

"Yes. Don’t worry, Your Majesty."

As Robins bowed, there was a sound from outside the door.

"The Five Stars of the Ivory Tower requests an audience."

The voice was urgent, and the royals stirred.

'Too soon?'

It was an opportunity for Thormia, but troubles often followed good news.

"Let them in."

As Robins quickly exited through the back door and the main door opened, King Adolph XIII stood up to greet Shirone.

"Welcome. A novice king greets the star of the Ivory Tower."

The sincerity in his actions and tone meant he was desperate.

"Yes. I am Shirone of the Ivory Tower."

"Apologies for the mess. As you can see, we’ve been through quite the turmoil."

‘Turmoil.’

While he might not know how many had died, Shirone took a seat without showing any signs.

Servants hurriedly brought in high-quality tea.

"It would be proper to offer a banquet, but there wasn’t time to prepare..."

"That’s fine."

He didn’t want to waste time.

"I came to hear the vision of Thormia’s new king. Humanity is in a precarious state."

"Of course."

As if what was expected had arrived, King Adolph XIII straightened his posture.

"Today, the Kingdom of Thormia has achieved an unprecedented victory in the world war. We have annihilated the demons and driven out the giants. It is a triumph accomplished together by the citizens of Thormia and the Holy War."

Shirone let that pass.

"We will announce this historic moment to the world. Thormia will become the focal point from which humanity will revive. Star of the Ivory Tower, please entrust me with this role."

'King Adolph XIII.' His real name was Adolph Tenar, and he was 44 years old.

Shirone knew his actions up to the age of 22 through Omega.

'From a young age, he was ambitious, and his problem-solving was typically quick, covert, and cruel.'

King Adolph XIII casually said,

"Thormia will do everything possible, with all its might and main, to assist the Five Stars." This might seem overt, but Shirone recalled what Goffin had left him.

-Listen to everyone's story.

"I understand."

King Adolph XIII sighed in relief.

'What? They say the Ivory Tower people are eccentric, but this one's actually a pushover?'

Hiding his thoughts, he said,

"If there's anything you need, just let me know. Anything at all."

"No. I’ve heard your vision, so I’ll be going. But where is Pony? She doesn’t seem to be here."

The royals' expressions stiffened slightly, and Shirone didn’t miss that fleeting change.

"Can you call her? I have something personal to discuss."

"Of course, follow up. Pony is truly a brave royal. She might be outside handling matters personally. As soon as she returns, she will..."

Shirone’s gaze sent a shiver down his spine.

It felt like being penetrated by a look.

'What’s this all of a sudden?'

He was seasoned in the political and social arenas with a poker face, but he had never felt so observed before.

Shirone asked,

"What are you hiding?"

King Adolph XIII, with a perfect smile, spoke,

"Hide? How could I speak falsehoods before the star of the Ivory Tower?"

Shirone chuckled.

"You still haven’t corrected that habit."

"What?"

"The unique smile you make when you lie."

"That’s absurd. I always smile."

"But in certain situations, you blink once. It’s a habit ingrained from childhood. Didn’t you once tell a lie and get a severe smack from the previous generation?"

The room fell silent, and King Adolph XIII gulped.

'How does he know that?'

No one knew that fact, except his father and himself.

'Would father even remember, if he were alive?'

It was such a minor event that even he had nearly forgotten it, but how could Shirone possibly know?

"I trained hard to perfect my smile, but there’s one thing you didn’t practice enough. After all..."

Shirone pointed at him.

"How many people could have given a smack to the prince of Thormia?"

King Adolph XIII’s fists on his knees trembled.

'Is the Five Star from the Universal Management Bureau a ghost?'

This wasn’t insight or analysis.

'It’s such a trivial thing. Even a mind-type magician would wander in my depths for a month to find it.'

He felt as if his soul had been stripped bare.

"One last chance. What are you hiding?"

Adolph took a gamble.

"Ha ha. I really don’t understand what you mean."

King Adolph XIII, sweating and smiling, closed his eyes again.

West Inferno Prison, Bashuka.

Upon receiving an urgent telegram, Pony handed over a document stamped with the royal seal to the warden.

"This way, please."

There is no citizen a king cannot govern, and every king of a state has the authority to grant pardons.

'I've been tasked with an unpleasant duty.'

Pony was preparing to leave Bashuka when she received an urgent call.

She was ordered to release a prisoner from Inferno, a person crucial for national restoration.

Knowing the situation was dire due to a shortage of personnel, she complied, but her disappointment in the power structure was as great as her skepticism.

"Please follow me."

She was greeted by the gloomiest, deepest facility in Block E of Inferno, where specially dangerous criminals were detained, each one vile.

'What kind of talent can be found in such a place?' Determined to leave for Alpheus Magic School once this task was completed, she walked down the long corridor. A woman's moan came from the room at the end.

While she knew mixed-gender housing was impossible, a man's breath interjected between the sultry voice of a woman.

'What kind of person is this?'

It was clear that this prisoner, said to be a talent, received special treatment.

However, already disgusted, her voice could only be cold.

"Prisoner number 3824, designated for special protection watch. Come out."

The moaning stopped abruptly.

'Special protection?'

It was one of the five keywords agreed upon with Prince Robins, meaning... Kill anyone who comes looking for you.

'The time has come.'

The warden's voice echoed from the ceiling.

"For 30 seconds, I will disable the magic control device in Block E. Any suspicious movement during the siren will be immediately executed by royal command."

The siren sounded and Pony felt the magic control device turn off.

"Brother, who's out there?"

Pony could no longer restrain herself at the sultry voice echoing through the criminal detention center.

"Come out, you bastard."

As she turned the key she received from the warden with a snap, she opened the door.

"Choke!"

Out of the darkness, a hand flew out and grabbed Pony's neck.

She couldn't resist as she was slammed against the prison wall with a loud bang.

"Damn you...!"

As she tried to cast a spell, an electric shock surged through her body.

"Gahhh!"

"Shush."

A familiar man with disheveled hair brought his face close and put a finger to his lips on her lips.

Pony's eyes widened.

"You?"

Electric Monster Lycon.

"Huh?"

Lycon also raised his drug-intoxicated lips in interest.

"Pony? Long time no see?"

"You, you bastard! Let go!"

The woman didn't bother to cover her naked body as she shook Lycon.

"Brother! Who is this woman? Don't tell me you called another woman besides me? You used to say I was the only one... Gah!"

As Lycon swung his hand, a thud sounded and the woman's face exploded.

'Incredible electric shock.'

"After all, you were royalty too. It's a pity. But take pride. You've grown enough to be a threat."

Lycon grinned, revealing rotten teeth.

"Die."

As electricity coiled around the hand tightening on her neck, Pony's eyes widened.

'Is that...?'

Behind Lycon, the figure of someone familiar was shining in golden light.

'Shirone?'
