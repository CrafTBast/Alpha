Day of Creator God’s Descent (2)

Overwhelming capital was like magic.

Jinwoo was an Archmage and had a lot of money. Thanks to that, he could present a fantastic magic show to everyone here. The showcase that was about to unfold would also be a magical moment.

Even until then, the cheers did not cease.

Rather, they only grew louder.

It was a perfect prayer.

That prayer was filled with joy and delight.

Jinwoo could feel that emotion overflow and fill the surroundings. For some reason, he could sense all of those emotions.

‘Saving the world with joy.’

That’s how it would be.

Hearing the prayer shouted by all the future Heroes together, he was filled with certainty.

Let us answer their prayer.

Let us become the developer that the Heroes desire.

That was the minimum courtesy he could offer to the Heroes who would dedicate themselves to Arsilan.

So, I shall accept your praises!

Jinwoo responded to that prayer and opened his arms. He slightly enchanted the lighting with magic to shine brighter on himself. As a result, the atmosphere on-site peaked to the point that no one could stop it anymore.

“Wow……”

Johnny felt goosebumps.

He had attended many events, but it was his first time experiencing joy that could be felt directly on the skin. Johnny, feeling awe, naturally straightened up with reverence in body and mind.

Even Johnny received a new script. Engulfed in many emotions, his body trembled, but he was a seasoned professional. He quickly calmed his emotions and grabbed the microphone.

“Haha! I got completely fooled, too.”

“Weren’t you informed?”

“No, I really had no idea. But I’m so glad I was fooled. Because I could feel the same emotions as our future Heroes!”

Johnny was known for having a loose mouth, so if he had been told in advance, it might have spoiled. Knowing that, it had been decided at the meeting. As Jinwoo sat down, the showcase officially began.

The viewer count skyrocketed as it was being broadcast not only on Arsilan Worlds but also on YouTube.

“Many people want to experience Episode 1 again. But unfortunately, past history cannot be reversed.”

At Jinwoo’s words, the future Heroes nodded in agreement.

Johnny had a good sense of things.

When Jinwoo smiled slightly, Johnny sensed something.

“Seeing you bring it up again like this, there must be something, right?”

“It’s the Tower of Trials.”

It was a tower that had previously been used as a Magic Tower. It had been left abandoned for being unnecessarily huge, but now it was being recycled this way.

Murmurs arose.

The eyes of the audience sparkled like stars in the night sky. When Jinwoo pressed the button again, a video began immediately.

The Goblin Subjugation battle appeared.

Leon and the people of Arsilan were not there, but the scene was exactly the same as back then. The Goblin Subjugation battle, the Defense of Lunatie, and even the Invasion of Erosion and the Lord of Erosion raid.

They were properly implemented.

“Though we can’t change the past, we can experience the trials. The Tower of Trials is a space where you can experience the past battlefields. Of course, they have been slightly adjusted according to the situation.”

Wooooaaah!

Cheers exploded!

“O Creator God!”

Johnny immediately knelt down and raised both hands high. Jinwoo smiled benevolently and stretched out his hand as if to bless him.

“The Goblin Subjugation will be held twice a week, the Defense of Lunatie and the Lord of Erosion Raid once a week, and the Invasion of Erosion will be held once every two weeks. To the Heroes who pass all the trials……”

The screen changed.

The emblem of the Golden Alliance of Arsilan appeared. It was different from the previous one, but it looked plenty cool.

“You will be able to wear this emblem on your chest, showing that we are one.”

You will be given the qualification to join the Golden Alliance of Arsilan!

Even the existing quests were integrated into the Tower of Trials.

The future Heroes were overwhelmed with emotion.

Some even shed tears of joy. Johnny, too, seemed moved and his eyes grew slightly red.

“But it will be very difficult. Do you think you’ll all be able to do well?”

“Of course! We can do it! Right?”

Wooooaaaah!

As Johnny looked out at the audience and said that, instead of a verbal response, a tremendous roar erupted. It was like the roar of a battlefield.

Jinwoo nodded with a smile.

Thanks to the magic core, it was possible to create multiple spaces and hold events like this. Though there would be a queue, instance dungeons were also possible.

From the start, the Erosion Core held a massive space large enough to cover that vast land. As long as there was Magic Power, this level of activity posed no burden.

The viewers’ reactions were explosive as well.

-They’re really giving us everything.

-I can’t believe we get to experience it like this!

-I’m tearing up…

-My faith is surging…

“The clear records will remain in the history of Arsilan forever. We will be giving out rewards such as trial-exclusive weapon skins and titles based on rankings, so please make sure to participate. Hmm, it looks like our senior Heroes will have to stay on their toes.”

The atmosphere in Arsilan Worlds was also heating up.

-Wow, they even prepared something like this.

-If we fall behind the Americans, it’ll be so embarrassing.

-They’ll never catch up to us ㅋㅋ

Rewards that only 1st place could use were updated monthly. If someone took first place, the weapon skin would be taken away too. This kind of healthy competition would be good for the Heroes’ growth.

The looks in their eyes changed.

The future Heroes were already ignited with competitive spirit.

“This was actually something we were planning to reveal later…, but with such a hot response, we can’t help it.”

Jinwoo immediately introduced the next content.

[Addition and Support for Production Classes]

“Even now, many people are devoted to production classes. They can be used alongside combat classes. Exclusive skills for production classes will also be added.”

The screen changed to reveal the names of various production classes.

There were specialized jobs that required qualification exams to change into. Alchemist, Magic Engineer, Magic Architect, Builder, and many more. They weren’t limited to choosing just one production class; they could register all of them freely.

“Several villages and cities will also be added.”

Jinwoo planned to abundantly fill the vast land.

As for cities, the plan was to restore ruins via the Magic Tower or completely relocate unoccupied cities to fill in. Even if not that, there would be plenty of other methods.

Jinwoo first took action.

Taking action first and cleaning up afterward. It was a trick he often used back when he was a game director.

“Now, I’d like to talk about something a bit more serious.”

When Jinwoo set the tone, it became quiet.

[The World We Build Together]

That phrase appeared on the screen.

“There are certainly inconveniences caused by language.”

Heroes from many countries were expected to participate together.

All systems would appear in their respective languages, but communication among them would surely be inconvenient.

“There is an interpretation magic. It’s a C-rank magic, so it will be quite difficult to learn. However, Arsilan natives can generally use interpretation magic, so there won’t be any inconvenience in daily life.”

He could cast interpretation magic on all Heroes, but he chose not to.

“Through interaction, the people of Arsilan may also learn your language. The same goes for you.”

Jinwoo wanted various cultures to slowly blend and create a new one.

“If you discover an ancient document during your travels, put your heads together and try to decode them. Catch dangerous monsters together or excavate hidden ruins. If misunderstandings arise, resolve them through duels.”

Everything was part of the romance that Arsilan Saga offered.

“Interacting with such a diverse group of Heroes, sharing fantastic experiences and exchanging cultures, will be an incredibly fascinating journey.”

A soft smile appeared on Jinwoo’s lips.

“The day will come when you won’t need words and can understand each other just through your gaze. As you adventure and fight together, you’ll influence one another. Don’t you think a beautiful culture of our own, of Arsilan’s own, will be born from such exchanges? So isn’t this truly the world we are building together?”

Clap, clap, clap!

Applause burst out.

Johnny also set down his microphone and clapped vigorously.

Everyone was becoming the protagonist, building new culture and history together!

Just that alone washed away all the lingering regrets they had held up until now. An inexplicable exhilaration surged up to the top of their heads.

Before them lay a land of adventure filled with infinite possibilities.

Now only one question remained in everyone’s mind. Johnny took up the microphone with a solemn expression.

“On behalf of everyone, I ask this. When exactly will you summon us to Arsilan?”

With all their hopes condensed into it, the question was asked to Jinwoo.

Silence fell over the convention center. It was so quiet, even breathing couldn’t be heard, as everyone focused on Jinwoo’s answer.

Jinwoo operated the remote control. Then, the Hero’s Flowers appeared on the display. As if waiting for their cue, the staff came up on stage holding Soul Links.

“This is Soul Link Light. The battery lasts for eight hours, and it can also be used while connected to a power source. It supports basic functions by linking to Ohsung Electronics smartphones or PCs.”

It was a version of the existing Soul Link with several features removed.

“When connected to the superintelligent AI, Ella, your senses are automatically adjusted. You won’t feel any latency based on distance.”

This was a newly introduced product lineup.

“This is Soul Link S, which can perform various functions independently without linking to a smartphone or PC. And this capsule is Soul Link U, which allows the longest possible connection time.”

Although there were differences in additional features, the performance required to enjoy Arsilan Saga was the same across the board. If budget was tight, the Soul Link Light with minimal functionality was the best choice.

So when is the release date?

Take all my money already!

The same applied to Korea. Now that there was not just enough but an abundance of Magic Stones, he planned to release the stock fully.

The U.S. version of the Soul Link was planned to be produced entirely at local U.S. factories, except for the previously manufactured inventory.

Even if they studied it for hundreds of years, they would never figure out how it worked.

One of those was the “Curse of Baldness”.
