This vast place was an empire in itself.

It felt like witnessing the prime of the Kartas Empire, the most powerful nation in Arsilan’s history.

Countless people were entering the hole, and they were coming back out with jewels or minerals. They didn’t look normal. Most of them were shriveled like mummies, working with great difficulty.

“Move!”

It seemed they were being devoured.

The soldiers herded them like livestock toward the city.

Their souls were completely corrupted, and at their core was the will of Erosion. That will was feeding on greed and sending it toward the massive city in the distance.

The accumulated greed was rising toward the sky, while the people’s suffering was being funneled into the deep abyss.
