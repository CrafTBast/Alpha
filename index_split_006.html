Chapter 56
Episode 1 (1)

This regular maintenance was scheduled to be longer than usual.

The maintenance was being conducted early Saturday morning, not on a usual maintenance day, and the server was scheduled to reopen after lunchtime. Since the regular maintenance had been postponed during the Lord of Erosion subjugation event, no one raised any complaints.

Even in Arsilan Worlds, only posts of encouragement were being uploaded.

All heroes were filled with anticipation and excitement.

During the subjugation event, Jinwoo had remained in the boss room the entire time, so it had been a while since he visited FS Games. With the relocation of the headquarters approaching, the interior of the building had been neatly organized.

As overseas service would soon begin, new employee recruitment had been significantly expanded. Since this narrow building could no longer accommodate everyone, they had purchased and remodeled an entire large-scale building. Thanks to Ohsung Group’s active involvement, Jinwoo had little to worry about.

Jinwoo conducted a meeting.

Although it was Saturday, most employees had come to work in preparation for the major event. Of course, he planned to compensate everyone later.

With real pay, not passion pay.

“Everything has been prepared locally. We plan to expand the service across North America, EU, and all of Asia according to the number of Soul Links.”

Jinwoo nodded at the report from Kim Yu-na, the head of the Business Strategy Team. There were still quite a few countries that had not lifted regulations on virtual reality. The United States had been the first to confirm, so the expansion was scheduled to start from there. The EU was also actively moving forward, and Japan as well.

“It really feels like this is just the beginning. Haha!”

Han Yong-seon said that and laughed.

Since Jinwoo first revealed virtual reality, they had really run without rest. They had shown many things and achieved many things. However, the path they had come so far was merely stepping out to the front door compared to the path they had to go forward.

“That’s right.”

Jinwoo nodded.

This was merely the starting point. It had taken a hundred years to reach this starting line.

The meeting continued in a cheerful atmosphere. Kim Yu-na and the staff brought something out.

“These are the goods to be revealed at this event. We also have some collaboration products.”

During the last online interaction, Jinwoo had promised a grand festival. Of course, preparations were underway, and he planned to hold offline events about twice a year. They would be carried out with the feel of “Arsilan Saga’s Summer and Winter Festivals.”

Jinwoo examined the goods.

There was a pile of Leon-related merchandise such as a Leon figure, Leon case, Leon photobook, and so on. They even had a collaboration with Ohsung Electronics’ smartphones and TVs.

Once Jinwoo gave permission, they had gone wild with production.

Wouldn’t people get tired of this at some point?

Jinwoo casually made such a comment.

Kim Yu-na firmly shook her head.

“There’s a saying that the more you see, the newer it feels. He looks cooler today than he did yesterday, and if you see him the next day, you’ll find a different charm again. That’s Leon.”

“Hmm…”

Jinwoo looked through the photobook. It featured Leon from the Early Access period to the present. There were also many unreleased photos. Seeing it like this, it did feel fresh.

Kim Yu-na showed a video.

Around the FS Games North America branch, people dressed in Arsilan Saga costumes filled the area.

“As soon as the visit schedule was announced, they all gathered.”

It wasn’t strange.

There had been similar responses in Korea. However, there was a more fervent aspect to it.

“It’s the Church of Arsilan’s Creator God.”

“What?”

“It started out as a meme, but it gradually grew to the point where it can now be called an organization.”

Jinwoo was dumbfounded.

They reportedly wore Arsilan robes and pledged every morning to fight Erosion. Starting with a summoning prayer, various other prayer were spreading.

“…This is a bit much.”

There were various religions, like the Flying Spaghetti Monster and such, but this felt a bit excessive. Not just a bit, it was way too much.

With talk of at least a million units of Soul Link being released, the fervor of worship grew even more intense.

Jinwoo stared at the video.

A news reporter approached an American wearing a costume.

[How long do you plan to wait here?]

(Until I become a Hero.)

He answered in Korean, and the reporter looked flustered.

[Excuse me? Please speak in English.]

[Ah! I accidentally spoke in Arsilanian.]

[That sounded like Korean?]

[Yes! It’s Korean and also the official language of Arsilan. It’s a must-know language for Heroes.]

[I-I see.]

The reporter was confused by his resolute tone but continued the interview. There were a few more videos like that.

A wave of national pride was rising.

Albeit in a slightly different form.

‘Hmm?’

Jinwoo briefly looked at his own hand. It seemed like a spark had flown, probably static electricity due to winter.

Jinwoo adjusted the upcoming schedule through the meeting, then returned to the Research Lab. He immediately headed for Arsilan. The Core of Erosion still floated in the boss room.

The Core of Erosion, dominated by Jinwoo’s magic power, was in constant pain. The energy of Erosion was swirling and being sucked into the core.

‘Once the Core of Erosion disappears, they’ll notice within a week at most.’

The Land of Erosion and the lands occupied by other entities were separated by spatial barriers. Fortunately, it would take time before the space reconnected. If he gathered the energy of Erosion and stationed it at the boundary, he could buy more time.

Jinwoo did the calculations through the Magic Tower.

He figured he could secure at least three months somehow. It was a tight schedule to secure and develop the Magic Stone Mine, build a Magic Tower, and deploy a large-scale Magic Barrier.

Still, he had managed to secure more time than expected.

‘It’ll be tough… ‘

But he was no longer alone.

Countless Heroes were on standby.

So now, he decided to enjoy the moment. He would put an end to the long-endured pain and move forward.

“I’ll become a ruthless invader.”

Invasion, war, plunder.

Unfortunately, those were the things humanity was best at.

Jinwoo pulled out several documents from subspace. They were collections of original artwork meticulously drawn by the Art Team. Depictions of the true boss that far surpassed the Lord of Erosion were drawn in multiple sheets.

Since this was the conclusion of Episode 1, a fairly imposing boss monster was needed. He planned to gather the remaining energy of Erosion and use it to create the monster.

“This one looks good.”

Jinwoo chose a monster that suited a true boss.

[Kraaargh! Aaaagh!]

“It’s just starting. Don’t start whining already.”

Jinwoo entered the ‘Episode 1 Ending’ notice board.

Jinwoo (Director): I’d like you to show your coolest side, if possible.

└Heina (Captain of the Guards): Understood.

└Helby (Magic Dragon): Hmph, I’ll play along.

└Tia (Unemployed): Hehe……

└Luceren (Elvens Village Chief): Ready and waiting.

└Garsha (Lunatie Village Chief): Standing by.

└Hallon (Dark Priest): Finally!

.

…

……

Everyone was ready!

Jinwoo immediately opened the server.

* * *

The Cabin of Memories.

That’s what the Heroes called it.

As soon as the server opened, it was filled with Heroes. Since it was located at a high altitude, they had to use Hero’s Flowers to reach it, but since the capacity was limited, not everyone could go.

Hellhound ran inside the cabin.

He had heard that new comments had been added to the photos. The cabin was packed with Heroes, and the queue was long. After a short wait, he was able to enter.

“Wow……”

“Sniff……”

The expressions on the Heroes’ faces varied.

Some smiled brightly while taking photos, while others wept uncontrollably. They were experiencing deep emotions that couldn’t be felt in reality.

Game? NPC?

None of that mattered.

Because the history they had gone through until now had truly existed.

Hellhound looked at the photo left by the Helby Lovers Group. At the very top, there was a message written by Helby herself.

“Ku……”

Hellhound was a Helby expert.

He could guess what feelings had gone into that message. Being acknowledged by someone is an incredibly joyful thing. All the more so if it’s someone you admire and respect more than anyone.

“Hoo……”

He heard someone breathing.

Hellhound turned to the side. The Northern Grand Duke was clutching the Heart Gem in her hand, letting out a deep sigh. Those who had already died could not leave messages here. He could somewhat understand how she must be feeling.

Drrrr!

The ground trembled as the entire cabin shook.

Hellhound and the Heroes rushed outside and looked toward the Lost Land. A black pillar erupted from the boss room, piercing the sky and swirling fiercely. The force grew stronger, and soon a black storm raged around the boss room.

Gugugugu!

A shape revealed itself in the sky.

A wicked figure shrouded in a black robe briefly appeared, and then an enormous hand that had tried to break through the Magic Barrier emerged from the sky.

Its size was so massive that it looked as though a mountain range was crashing down.

“Crazy……”

Those words came out involuntarily.

Hellhound was utterly dazed by the overwhelming sight. The same went for the other Heroes.

From the Hero’s Flowers scattered across the Lost Land, bright light gushed forth, trying to push the giant hand back upward. The giant hand resisted and reached out toward the earth.

Craack!

With unbelievable ease, the massive hand smashed the boss room to pieces. And it didn’t stop there. Its fingers pierced into the ground. That alone caused a quake, shaking the entire area violently.

The giant hand gripped the earth as if to rip something out and rose upward.

Kwoooaaargh!

What it held was a black skull.

Like looking at a mountain, the massive upper body of a black skull emerged from the ground. The hand that pulled it up rose into the sky, pushed by the light from the Hero’s Flowers, and then disappeared.

Boom!

When the black skull raised its hand and slammed it down, fragments of the ground scattered in every direction. Golden liquid flowed from the eyes and jaw of the black skull. It looked as though the remnants of Arsilan it had devoured were now being vomited back out.

The black skull began to drag its body across the ground. It crawled forward, extending its massive hand and clawing at the earth. It looked slow due to its sheer size, but it was not slow at all. It was a monster incomparably beyond the Lord of Erosion.

“T-This big thing they mentioned……”

“So this is what they meant?”

The Heroes muttered blankly, staring at the monster.

The real big one had shown up.

It was so enormous that if the skull stretched out its hand, it felt as if it could touch the edge of the sky.

“Ah……”

Hellhound looked in the direction the black skull was headed.

“T-The Magic Barrier!”

The black skull was crawling toward the Magic Barrier. Beyond that barrier lay Evanth and the realm of Arsilan.

“Gather up, now!”

“Damn it…! How many users are logged in right now?”

Thankfully, it was Saturday.

The Heroes quickly began spreading the word.

Hellhound also called together the Helby Lovers Group and moved down. Once the Helby Lovers Group was fully assembled, they moved toward the black skull.

“Wow…, this is insane.”

“I’m speechless.”

There had been some criticism in Arsilan Worlds about the Lord of Erosion, some said it felt a bit underwhelming. Some said he lacked the presence of a final boss. Toward the end, he was being taken down in three minutes, so it was understandable.

Did Creator God hear those complaints?

This was on a completely different level.

Looking up at the black skull from below was simply breathtaking. It looked like a moving island. The sound of it scraping the ground as it crawled felt like it could rupture eardrums.

Word must have spread fast, as the number of logins was rising rapidly.

“Attack!”

“Protect the Magic Barrier!”

“Damn it, fuck! Just throw everything you’ve got at it!”

Crraackkk!

Countless Heroes gathered around and unleashed all their attacks toward the black skull.

Unlike the Lord of Erosion, this black skull didn’t use magic. It didn’t emit the energy of Erosion either. It possessed only one thing, overwhelming physical mass.

Guoooooo!

When the black skull swung its hand, hundreds of Heroes were flung away and vanished in an instant. Even the blackened ground was torn up as if its surface were being peeled away.

Nearly all the logged-in Heroes had gathered, pouring attacks on the black skull.

Crack!

A fracture formed in the black skull’s bones, and golden liquid began to flow out. Wherever that liquid touched the ground, the blackened soil disappeared, revealing the Lost Land beneath.

The Heroes instinctively realized.

If they destroyed the black skull, all the Erosion covering the Lost Land would vanish.

However.

“How are we supposed to stop that thing!”

“It’s already right in front of the Magic Barrier…!”

Forget destroying it, no one could even think of a way to stop it.

Though the Heroes gave it everything they had, the black skull’s pace didn’t slow. Near the Magic Barrier, it pressed forward, tanking the attacks head-on.

A huge crowd of Heroes had gathered in front of the Magic Barrier.

There were more than 30,000 of them, but they looked pitiful before the black skull. Many had already fallen trying to stop it and were now waiting to respawn.

Hellhound, who joined the front line at the barrier, clenched his teeth.

“Uwaaaah! We’ll protect the Evanth Platinum Coin Shop!”

“Uwaaaaaah!”

“Protect Helby-nim!”

As the Helby Lovers Group raised their spirits, the surrounding Heroes also tried to muster their morale somehow. But the cheer began to fade in front of the black skull’s sheer presence.

The black skull stopped in front of the Magic Barrier.

The skull placed its hand on the ground and lifted its body upright.

In that moment, a shadow loomed over the Heroes.

The red sky shining through the gaps in its bones looked like blood gushing out.

Gwooooo!

The black skull raised both arms as if it were about to charge the Magic Barrier. Just as it was about to hurl itself forward.

Whoooosh!

A wave of light rippled from behind.

The light caused the black skull to hesitate and falter momentarily.

“Huh?”

Hellhound and the Heroes looked back. A white streak had appeared across the golden-glimmering Magic Barrier.

Arsilan!

It was light flowing in from Arsilan, beyond the Magic Barrier.

“The Magic Barrier is…”

“I-It’s opening?”

The light grew even stronger.

The rift in the Magic Barrier widened, and the scenery of Arsilan appeared.

The Magic Barrier that had separated Arsilan and the Land of Erosion began to dissolve, and the beautiful light of Arsilan poured in. Beyond that, countless Arsilan citizens stood gathered.

“You’re not touching my field!”

“It took forever to restore that land!”

Even members of the Farming Alliance stood with them, holding farming tools in hand.

Hellhound got goosebumps. No doubt the other Heroes felt the same.

“Guild Master! Up there!”

One member of the Helby Lovers Group pointed to the sky, and Hellhound raised his head to look.

There, Helby stood floating in the air with her arms crossed.

“H-H-H-H-H-H-Helby-nim?”

Kwooooooo!

The black skull slammed its arms into the ground with immense force. At the same time, its massive torso lunged toward Helby and the people of Arsilan.

[Who’s protecting whom.]

Fwoooosh!

Light exploded from Helby’s body.

All the colors of the world seemed to exist within her. An overwhelming magic power surged from her, enough to tear through flesh, distorting the vision of the Heroes.

As Helby’s body rippled with golden light.

KWA-GA-GA-GA-GA!

A storm of magic burst outward, and her body began to transform.

“Uwooooooo!”

“U-Uwaaaaah!”

Hellhound and the Helby Lovers Group erupted into wild cheers at the sight. Emerging from the storm of magic was a colossal magic dragon that held galaxies within and created starlight.

The magic dragon opened her mouth.

A vast amount of starlight gathered inside her jaws. It swirled violently, forming another sun.

When that light reached its peak, the starlight descended.

KWAGAGAGAGAGA!

The breath of starlight that burst forth surged toward the black skull. The black skull was pushed back forcefully and hurled far into the distance.

The magic dragon dominated the sky, painting the crimson sky in her own color.

“M-Master!”

“That’s our Master!”

Helby had now been exalted as the Master of the Helby Lovers Group!

The Magic Barrier fully opened and crumbled away. As its light touched the ground, Hero’s Flowers bloomed.

[Ella has placed her faith in the Heroes and withdrawn the Magic Barrier.]

[Ella’s Radiant Blessing now imbues all Heroes and their Hero’s Flowers. For today, the resurrection wait time is gone. Become the unwavering hope that drives Erosion from this land!]

[S] Ella’s Radiant Blessing

She has withdrawn the Magic Barrier and passed her power to the Heroes.

You are now the barrier of hope that will expel Erosion.

Become the endlessly reviving hope born from the Hero’s Flowers, and move forward!

Fwaaash!

All the Heroes who had fallen to the black skull were revived through the Hero’s Flowers.

Fwooooo!

The Heroes pulled out their horns and blew them.

Countless Heroes stood side by side with the people of Arsilan, gazing at the fallen black skull.

“Let’s reclaim our land!”

“For Arsilan!”

Charge!
