Great War (1)

Arsilan was peaceful.

The cities and villages were full of vitality, and even the cloudy weather felt warm and beautiful. However, the change that occurred at the edge of the boundary constantly made the hearts of the Heroes uneasy.

It was impossible not to notice.

The end of the boundary always flickered ominously, and at some point, that flickering intensified and began to form something enormous.

It was a massive gate.

It reached as high as the sky, and its width was enough to swallow an entire village and more. Gradually, the hazy gate became clearer, tearing through space to reveal itself.

Gate of Invasion.

The gate looked as though it was made of bones. The pillars of the gate seemed to be built with bricks, but through the broken gaps beyond space, countless bones could be seen.

They looked like the bones of giants, or perhaps like the skulls of monsters. It might even have been the bones of Arsilan’s people.

“Ah…”

“Crazy…”

The Heroes stopped what they were doing and blankly stared at the scene. From the broken gaps, black dark clouds leaked out, horrifically decorating the Gate of Invasion.

Everyone knew that Arsilan Saga was a world inside a game, a fantasy world, but even so, that scene was just too unrealistic.

It was beyond the realm of understanding for the Heroes. So it felt like a dream.

The Heroes hurriedly turned on their broadcast or started sending messages.

Bones sprouted from the pillars of the gate, and the black dark clouds turned into bandages. In an instant, enormous arms were formed. That appearance resembled the melted hands that had appeared when the Lord of Erosion invaded in the past.

Creeeak!

The massive arms bent inward and reached for the tightly shut gate. It appeared to move slowly, but it was incredibly fast. As the air and magic collided and compressed around the arm, flames flared up. The arm bent inward completely, and its grotesquely long fingers scraped across the surface of the gate.

Kyaaaaaaaaak!

Kuaaaaaaaak!

As the fingers scraped the surface like nails on a chalkboard, a horrific noise echoed. That noise sounded like a scream.

“This is…”

“Yeah, it’s definitely dark fantasy.”

“Ugh, yeah.”

The Heroes murmured blankly as they watched the scene.

Arsilan was a precarious world on the brink of destruction. It had been so peaceful for a while that they had forgotten that fact. Heroes who had joined from Episode 2 didn’t even know about it.

It was when the massive hand gripped the surface of the gate as if to tear it open.

A huge blue chain sprang up from the ground, grabbing the arm and wrapping around the gate. The massive arm trembled violently in resistance, but chains continued to emerge, wrapping around the gate as if sealing it.

Before long, both arms were wrapped in chains along with the gate.

Boom!

A pillar of light shot up from the village of Arsilan. In the capital Solis and the international city Marogo, even greater lights burst forth, brightly coloring the surroundings. The dark clouds flowing out from the Gate of Invasion were being disintegrated by the light. Nevertheless, the sky continued to darken.

[The Gate of Invasion has appeared, tearing through space.]

[Arsilan] Prelude to the Great War
