For the Heroes, the Empire of Evil was a lawless land where everything was permitted.

The nobles and ruling class of the Empire of Evil mostly had bloated physiques that resembled monsters, and their soldiers were so close to mummies that they looked like another race. The more greed accumulated in their souls, the more they showed such appearances. Both in terms of appearance and in-game setting, they were corrupted and mutated beings, so it was fair to consider them as another race.

In just two weeks, over hundreds of thousands of Heroes had entered Episode 3. It was a much faster pace than expected.

Although this number was extremely small in perspective of the entire Empire of Evil, the power and potential each Hero possessed were tremendous.

From the enemy’s point of view, they were disasters incarnate.

Cities and villages bordering the desert were completely devastated. The treasures offered by the Heroes were stored in the Soul Gate, and after absorbing the energy of Erosion and power imbued in the treasures, they were sent to the warehouse.

‘This is truly automatic hunting.’

Even without doing anything, the Soul Gate was being filled with power. Jinwoo was slowly stealing the strength built up by the Lord of Greed through the Heroes.

“A new Domain has been created!”

“Oh, that’s already the fourth one.”

Jinwoo was in FS Games’ control room, checking various indicators.

Including Romance, four Domains had been established.

Even without Jinwoo adding any extra content, the Empire of Evil itself became content. Routes were connected between each Domain, and trade ships actively came and went. Many Heroes even specialized entirely in sailing.

Whatever one did, it was free. Just by pursuing fun, the Heroes greatly helped Arsilan.

Arsilan Saga was a game available only to adults.

The adult content that was supposed to be added in Episode 1 had been delayed until Episode 3. It was postponed due to various regulations and the need to coordinate with the circumstances of multiple countries.

There had been a movement in several Korean organizations to impose restrictions, but it was quietly buried. The number of Heroes was simply too large for such minor public opinion to sway anything. Arsilan Saga had now grown far too massive to be limited by regulation.

Finally, adult content that could be engaged in with consent in Safe Zones was added. At the same time, various paid items were released, and the stat “Libido” was added to the system.

“Adult content revenue is… truly incredible.”

“It easily surpassed the sales of the Platinum Coin Shop.”

Jinwoo nodded.

‘You guys…, you were really holding back a lot.’

Heroes were truly sacred.

Aside from the adult content, the most attention-grabbing content right now was the Underground Labyrinth. The city deep within had heard word of the invasion and was now heavily fortified. It had become difficult to conquer even with an expeditionary unit, but since rare treasures could be found in the Underground Labyrinth, many Heroes were gathering there.

Most of all, soloing and party hunting were both possible, so Heroes who weren’t fond of invasion, occupation, or plundering could enjoy it without pressure.

The Underground Labyrinth stirred the spirit of adventure in the Heroes.

From the perspective of the Lord of Greed, it was like immortal insects entering his mouth and cutting his teeth and gums. The defensive mechanisms hastily activated by the Lord of Greed only created an even more impressive labyrinth.

The structures created to feed off the pain of slaves also turned into very entertaining traps.

‘Wow, the response changes based on the number of people.’

‘Found a hole leading to the second floor, but missed it right in front of my eyes.’

‘Only tricky monsters appear. It feels like they’re reading my gear and reacting accordingly?’

‘The traps keep changing. Seriously, the design is insane. Praise the Creator God!’

‘The Underground Labyrinth… my dopamine… ‘

From a game design perspective, the structure was excellent. There were even aspects that Jinwoo hadn’t thought of himself.

The air vents dug open by the Lord of Greed to manifest his power had become paths leading downward, and the continuous traps that approached to kill the intruders provided great thrills to the Heroes.

The path leading further down in the Underground Labyrinth opened periodically.

It operated on a similar principle to the Lord of Greed trying to block his nose and mouth to stop the invasion, but eventually being unable to endure and opening his mouth, allowing the invaders in.

Up to this point, Jinwoo had thoroughly observed the Underground Labyrinth and uploaded it to the update log.
