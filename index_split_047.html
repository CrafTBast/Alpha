Offline? (2)

Jinwoo was doing a lot for Earth. It was because Earth needed to be healthy for the Heroes to enjoy Arsilan Saga without worry. He made many donations through FS Games and created several foundations to help protect Earth.

For the parts that couldn’t be solved with money, he stepped in secretly. He removed things that could become a threat to Earth in advance and prevented natural disasters that could cause significant damage. Since the natural disasters disappeared so abruptly right before hitting, many scholars began studying them.

‘Heroes should be happy.’

Jinwoo always thought that way.

Children were future Heroes, youths were spirited Heroes, and middle-aged adults were seasoned Heroes. Elderly people in their twilight years were Heroes with abundant experience and wisdom.

Jinwoo used Soul Link to heal those who used it or breathe vitality into them, and even secretly cured incurable diseases. Sometimes, he cast powerful luck-enhancing magic to help them avoid accidents.

That’s how precious Heroes were to him.

When he saw posts saying that people became happy after becoming Heroes in Arsilan Worlds, he felt proud.

Even someone like Jinwoo, however, completely excluded certain people from the list of those to protect.

They were the ones who, despite becoming Heroes through Soul Link, committed all sorts of atrocities and were eventually ‘banned’. As the number of Heroes surpassed five million and approached ten million, the number of banned users also steadily increased.

They tried every method possible to reconnect to Arsilan Saga, but not once did they succeed. The brand marked on their souls could never be erased, and they could never become Heroes again.

‘If they had kept quiet, I would have left them alone.’

They began gathering through specific social media platforms and started spreading all sorts of conspiracy theories about Arsilan Saga. They even slandered famous Heroes. Their posts were filled with the confidence that no one could ever catch them.

When the Comfortable Expedition Team became popular this time, it was also them who spread all kinds of slander and baseless rumors. Because of their organized activities, quite a few others ended up getting swept up by them.

‘Looks like it’s time for some management.’

Naturally, he had already identified every one of them. He would take legal measures and also punish them personally. He could tolerate people bad-mouthing him to some extent, but harming Arsilan Saga and its Heroes was something he could never forgive.

The reason Arsilan Worlds remained clean and free of chaos wasn’t just because FS Games managed it well, it was also thanks to Jinwoo’s intervention. It was time to clean up those causing trouble outside of Arsilan Worlds as well.

“Well then……”

Deep within the lab, Jinwoo began crafting a physical body to use for this mission. He didn’t need to build everything from scratch and instead modified the body that Tiris had given him to suit Korea.

As he worked, he started enjoying it and added various things.

“Hmm.”

Jinwoo nodded as he looked at the body standing before him. Since it was modified based on Tiris’s body, it was naturally an unusually handsome face for Earth.

He added muscles that could easily crush tanks and upgraded the skeleton to match. He even engraved a magic circle that radiated pressure and charisma.

“Excellent.”

It was completely different from the gentle appearance of Comfortable.

Standing in front of him was a manly figure exuding an aura like he could beat monsters to death right now. The mixed-race impression made it even more striking. He looked like someone born between an Arsilan and a Korean.

Creating a new identity wasn’t difficult.

While protecting Earth, he had already prepared various means, and he only needed to use them slightly.

His name was Lee Pyeon-an, and he was 28 years old.

‘Feels kind of like Earth Online.’

Jinwoo let out a small laugh.

For Jinwoo, Arsilan was offline, and Earth was essentially online. After placing his original body safely on the bed in the lab, Jinwoo inhabited the new one.

He put on the prepared clothes and looked into the mirror.

Just by standing still, he radiated overwhelming presence. He gave off the energy of someone who could survive in a apocalypse with just his two fists. As he nodded and slightly flexed his body.

“Ah.”

Riiip!

His muscles twitched, and the clothes tore apart instantly.

It seemed some adjustments were still necessary.
